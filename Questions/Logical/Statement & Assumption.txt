Statement & Assumption

Directions (Q1-Q20): In each of the following questions, a statement is given followed by two assumptions numbered I and II. An assumption is something supposed or taken for granted. You have to consider the statement and the following assumptions and decide which of the assumptions is implicit in the statement.

Q1. Statement: "Please drop this letter in the letterbox." - An officer tells his assistant.
Assumptions:
I. The assistant knows the location of the letterbox.
II. The assistant will follow the instructions.
A) Only assumption I is implicit
B) Only assumption II is implicit
C) Neither I nor II is implicit
D) Both I and II are implicit
Answer: D) Both I and II are implicit

Q2. Statement: "If you want to study Accounting, join Institute Y." - A advice given to B.
Assumptions:
I. Institute Y provides good accounting education.
II. B listens to A's advice.
A) Only assumption I is implicit
B) Only assumption II is implicit
C) Neither I nor II is implicit
D) Both I and II are implicit
Answer: D) Both I and II are implicit
