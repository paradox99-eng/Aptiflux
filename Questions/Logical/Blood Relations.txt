Blood Relations

Q1. Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "I have no brother or sister but that man's father is my father's son." Whose photograph was it?
A) His own
B) His son's
C) His father's
D) His grandfather's
Answer: B) His son's

Q2. Pointing to a photograph of a boy, Suresh said, "He is the son of the only son of my mother." How is Suresh related to that boy?
A) Brother
B) Uncle
C) Cousin
D) Father
Answer: D) Father

Q3. Introducing a boy, a girl said, "He is the son of the daughter of the father of my uncle." How is the boy related to the girl?
A) Brother
B) Nephew
C) Uncle
D) Son-in-law
Answer: A) Brother

Q4. A is the mother of B and C. If D is the husband of C, what is A to D?
A) Mother
B) Sister
C) Aunt
D) Mother-in-law
Answer: D) Mother-in-law

Q5. Pointing to a woman, a man said, "Her father is the only son of my father." How is the man related to the woman?
A) Brother
B) Father
C) Grandfather
D) Uncle
Answer: B) Father

Q6. A man points to a lady and says, "The son of her only brother is the brother of my wife." How is the lady related to the man?
A) Mother's sister
B) Grandmother
C) Sister of father-in-law
D) Mother-in-law
Answer: C) Sister of father-in-law

Q7. If A + B means A is the brother of B; A x B means A is the father of B; A ÷ B means A is the mother of B, which of the following would mean M is the son of P?
A) P x M ÷ N
B) P + M x N
C) P ÷ M + N
D) P x M + N
Answer: D) P x M + N

Q8. A is the father of B. C is the daughter of B. D is the brother of B. E is the son of A. What is the relationship between C and E?
A) Brother and sister
B) Cousins
C) Niece and uncle
D) Aunt and nephew
Answer: C) Niece and uncle

Q9. Pointing to a gentleman, Deepak said, "His only brother is the father of my daughter's father." How is the gentleman related to Deepak?
A) Grandfather
B) Father
C) Brother-in-law
D) Uncle
Answer: D) Uncle

Q10. If X is the brother of the son of Y's son, how is X related to Y?
A) Son
B) Brother
C) Grandson
D) Nephew
Answer: C) Grandson

Q11. Introducing a woman, a man said, "She is the only daughter of my wife's grandfather." How is the woman related to the man?
A) Mother
B) Mother-in-law
C) Sister
D) Aunt
Answer: B) Mother-in-law

Q12. Q's mother is the sister of P and daughter of M. S is the daughter of P and sister of T. How is M related to T?
A) Grandmother or Grandfather
B) Father or Mother
C) Uncle or Aunt
D) Brother or Sister
Answer: A) Grandmother or Grandfather

Q13. A is B's sister. C is B's mother. D is C's father. E is D's mother. Then, how is A related to D?
A) Grandmother
B) Grandfather
C) Daughter
D) Granddaughter
Answer: D) Granddaughter

Q14. Pointing to a boy, a woman said, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the woman related to the boy?
A) Mother
B) Sister
C) Aunt
D) Grandmother
Answer: A) Mother

Q15. P is the brother of Q and R. S is R's mother. T is P's father. Which of the following statements cannot be definitely true?
A) T is Q's father
B) S is P's mother
C) P is S's son
D) Q is T's son
Answer: D) Q is T's son

Q16. A man said to a lady, "Your mother's husband's sister is my aunt." How is the lady related to the man?
A) Daughter
B) Granddaughter
C) Mother
D) Sister
Answer: D) Sister

Q17. Pointing to a photograph, a lady tells Ram, "I am the only daughter of this lady and her son is your maternal uncle." How is the speaker related to Ram's father?
A) Sister-in-law
B) Wife
C) Sister
D) Mother
Answer: B) Wife

Q18. A family consists of six members A, B, C, D, E and F. B is the son of C but C is not the mother of B. A and C are a married couple. E is the brother of C. D is the daughter of A. F is the brother of A. Who is the mother of B?
A) A
B) C
C) D
D) E
Answer: A) A

Q19. In the family described in Q18, how many male members are there?
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 5
Answer: C) 4

Q20. Pointing to a person, a man said to a woman, "His mother is the only daughter of your father." How was the woman related to the person?
A) Aunt
B) Mother
C) Wife
D) Daughter
Answer: B) Mother
