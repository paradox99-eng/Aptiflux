Directions (Q1-Q5): Read the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Six people A, B, C, D, E, and F live on six different floors of a building. The ground floor is numbered 1, the floor above it is 2, and so on up to the top floor numbered 6.
1) C lives on an even-numbered floor but not on the top floor.
2) Only two people live between C and E.
3) A lives immediately above E.
4) B lives on an odd-numbered floor but not on the ground floor.
5) D lives on a floor higher than F.

Q1. Who lives on the top floor (Floor 6)?
A) E
B) A
C) D
D) B
Answer: B) A

Q2. How many people live between B and F?
A) None
B) One
C) Two
D) Three
Answer: B) One

Q3. On which floor does D live?
A) 2nd floor
B) 3rd floor
C) 4th floor
D) 5th floor
Answer: C) 4th floor

Q4. Who lives immediately below C?
A) B
B) D
C) E
D) F
Answer: D) F

Q5. Which of the following pairs represents the people living on the even-numbered floors (from bottom to top)?
A) C, D, A
B) F, C, E
C) B, C, A
D) F, B, E
Answer: A) C, D, A


Directions (Q6-Q10): Read the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Five subjects—Math, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and English—are taught from Monday to Friday, one subject per day.
1) Chemistry is taught on Thursday.
2) Math is taught immediately before Biology.
3) Physics is taught after English.
4) English is not taught on Monday.

Q6. Which subject is taught on Monday?
A) Math
B) English
C) Physics
D) Biology
Answer: A) Math

Q7. Which subject is taught immediately after Chemistry?
A) English
B) Math
C) Physics
D) Biology
Answer: C) Physics

Q8. On which day is English taught?
A) Tuesday
B) Wednesday
C) Thursday
D) Friday
Answer: B) Wednesday

Q9. How many subjects are taught between Math and Chemistry?
A) None
B) One
C) Two
D) Three
Answer: C) Two

Q10. Which of the following statements is true based on the schedule?
A) Biology is taught on Wednesday
B) English is taught on Tuesday
C) Physics is taught on Friday
D) Math is taught on Thursday
Answer: C) Physics is taught on Friday

Q14. If P and S exchange their shirts, what color shirt will S wear?
A) Yellow

Q15. Which color shirt does Q wear?
A) Yellow
B) Green

Directions (Q16-Q20): Read the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
A committee of 3 members is to be formed from a group of 3 men (M1, M2, M3) and 3 women (W1, W2, W3) with the following conditions:
1) The committee must have exactly two men and one woman, OR exactly two women and one man.
2) M1 cannot be on the same committee as W1.
3) M2 and W2 must always be selected together.
4) W3 cannot be selected if M3 is selected.

Q16. Which of the following members must definitely be in any valid committee formed?
A) M1
B) M2
C) W1
D) W3
Answer: B) M2

Q17. If M3 is selected for the committee, who must be the other two members?
A) M1 and W2
B) M2 and W1
C) M2 and W2
D) W1 and W2
Answer: C) M2 and W2

Q18. How many different combinations of the committee are possible?
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 5
Answer: C) 4

Q19. If W1 is selected for the committee, who are the other two members?
A) M1 and M2
B) M2 and W2
C) M3 and W2
D) W2 and W3
Answer: B) M2 and W2

Q20. Which of the following combinations is NOT a valid committee?
A) M1, M2, W2
B) M2, M3, W2
C) M2, W1, W2
D) M1, M3, W2
Answer: D) M1, M3, W2
