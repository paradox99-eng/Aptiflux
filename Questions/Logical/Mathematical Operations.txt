Mathematical Operations

Q1. If '+' means '-', '-' means '*', '*' means '/' and '/' means '+', then what is the value of: 10 * 2 / 5 - 3 + 4 ?
A) 12
B) 16
C) 20
D) 24
Answer: B) 16

Q2. If 'P' denotes 'multiplied by', 'T' denotes 'subtracted from', 'M' denotes 'added to' and 'B' denotes 'divided by', then 28 B 7 P 8 T 6 M 4 = ?
A) 28
B) 30
C) 32
D) 34
Answer: B) 30

Q3. If 'x' means '+', '-' means 'x', '/' means '-' and '+' means '/', then what is the value of: 15 - 5 / 20 + 4 x 6 ?
A) 68
B) 72
C) 76
D) 82
Answer: C) 76

Q4. If 'A' stands for '+', 'B' stands for '-', 'C' stands for '*' and 'D' stands for '/', then what is the value of: 16 D 4 C 3 A 8 B 2 ?
A) 12
B) 16
C) 18
D) 20
Answer: C) 18

Q5. If '+' means 'divided by', '-' means 'added to', '*' means 'subtracted from' and '/' means 'multiplied by', then what is the value of: 24 / 12 - 18 + 9 * 5 ?
A) 275
B) 280
C) 285
D) 290
Answer: C) 285

Q6. Which of the following interchanges of signs would make the given equation correct? 5 + 3 x 8 - 12 / 4 = 3
A) + and -
B) - and /
C) + and x
D) x and /
Answer: B) - and /

Q7. If 'L' stands for '+', 'M' stands for '-', 'N' stands for 'x', 'P' stands for '/', then 14 N 10 L 42 P 2 M 8 = ?
A) 141
B) 153
C) 160
D) 165
Answer: B) 153

Q8. If '-' stands for addition, '+' stands for subtraction, '/' stands for multiplication and '*' stands for division, then which one of the following equations is correct?
A) 20 * 4 - 6 + 2 / 5 = 1
B) 20 - 4 + 6 * 2 / 5 = 1
C) 20 + 4 / 6 - 2 * 5 = 1
D) 20 / 4 - 6 * 2 + 5 = 1
Answer: A) 20 * 4 - 6 + 2 / 5 = 1

Q18. If P = 6, J = 4, L = 8, M = 24, then which of the following is true?
