Venn Diagrams

Directions: For each question, select the option that best describes the Venn diagram representing the relationships between the given classes.

Q1. Which of the following Venn diagrams best represents the relationship between the classes: "Weeks, Days, Years"?
A) Three concentric circles (one inside the other).
B) Three disjoint circles.
C) Two disjoint circles inside a larger circle.
D) Two intersecting circles inside a larger circle.
Answer: A) Three concentric circles (one inside the other).

C) Three intersecting circles.
D) Two disjoint circles inside a larger circle.
Answer: B) One circle inside another, and a third circle intersecting both.
