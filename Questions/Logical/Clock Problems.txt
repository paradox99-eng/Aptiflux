Clock Problems

Q1. Find the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock when the time is 3:25.
A) 45°
B) 47.5°
C) 50°
D) 52.5°
Answer: B) 47.5°

Q2. At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock be exactly together?
A) 20 2/11 min past 4
B) 21 9/11 min past 4
C) 22 2/11 min past 4
D) 23 1/11 min past 4
Answer: B) 21 9/11 min past 4

Q3. How many times in a day are the hands of a clock in a straight line (either coincident or opposite)?
A) 22
B) 24
C) 44
D) 48
Answer: C) 44

Q4. What is the angle between the two hands of a clock when the time is 8:30?
A) 60°
B) 75°
C) 85°
D) 90°
Answer: B) 75°

Q5. How many times in a day do the hands of a clock coincide?
A) 11
B) 22
C) 24
D) 44
Answer: B) 22

Q6. At what time between 7 and 8 o'clock will the hands of a clock be in the same straight line but not together?
A) 5 5/11 min past 7
B) 6 5/11 min past 7
C) 7 5/11 min past 7
D) 8 5/11 min past 7
Answer: A) 5 5/11 min past 7

Q7. Find the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock when the time is 10:40.
A) 70°
B) 75°
C) 80°
D) 85°
Answer: C) 80°

Q8. How many times are the hands of a clock at a right angle in a day?
A) 22
B) 24
C) 44
D) 48
Answer: C) 44

Q9. What is the angle between the two hands of a clock when the time is 4:20?
A) 10°
B) 15°
C) 20°
D) 25°
Answer: A) 10°

Q10. At what angle are the hands of a clock inclined at 15 minutes past 5?
A) 58.5°
B) 64°
C) 67.5°
D) 72.5°
Answer: C) 67.5°

Q11. An accurate clock shows 8 o'clock in the morning. Through how many degrees will the hour hand rotate when the clock shows 2 o'clock in the afternoon?
A) 144°
B) 150°
C) 168°
D) 180°
Answer: D) 180°

Q12. The angle between the minute hand and the hour hand of a clock when the time is 4:15 is:
A) 32.5°
B) 35°
C) 37.5°
D) 40°
Answer: C) 37.5°

Q13. How many times do the hands of a clock point opposite to each other in a day?
A) 11
B) 12
C) 22
D) 24
Answer: C) 22

Q14. At what time between 9 and 10 o'clock will the hands of a watch be together?
A) 45 min past 9
B) 49 1/11 min past 9
C) 50 min past 9
D) 51 3/11 min past 9
Answer: B) 49 1/11 min past 9

Q15. A clock is started at noon. By 10 minutes past 5, the hour hand has turned through:
A) 145°
B) 150°
C) 155°
D) 160°
Answer: C) 155°

Q16. What is the reflex angle between the hands of a clock at 10:25?
A) 162.5°
B) 187.5°
C) 197.5°
D) 200.5°
Answer: C) 197.5°

Q17. At what time between 5:30 and 6 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles?
A) 40 min past 5
B) 43 7/11 min past 5
C) 45 min past 5
D) 47 5/11 min past 5
Answer: B) 43 7/11 min past 5

Q18. The angle between the hands of a clock at 3:40 is:
A) 120°
B) 125°
C) 130°
D) 135°
Answer: C) 130°

Q19. How much time does a watch lose per day, if its hands coincide every 64 minutes?
A) 32 8/11 min
B) 36 5/11 min
C) 90 min
D) 96 min
Answer: A) 32 8/11 min

Q20. What will be the acute angle between the hands of a clock at 2:30?
A) 95°
B) 105°
C) 115°
D) 120°
Answer: B) 105°
