Seating Arrangement

Directions (Q1-Q5): Read the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Six friends P, Q, R, S, T, and U are sitting in a circle facing the center.
1) P is sitting between T and S.
2) U is sitting between Q and R.
3) T is sitting to the immediate right of Q.

Q1. Who is sitting to the immediate left of R?
A) S
B) U
C) P
D) T
Answer: A) S

Q2. Who is sitting opposite to U?
A) P
B) Q
C) R
D) S
Answer: A) P

Q3. Who is sitting opposite to Q?
A) P
B) R
C) S
D) T
Answer: C) S

Q4. Who is sitting exactly between P and Q?
A) R
B) S
C) T
D) U
Answer: C) T

Q5. What is the position of R with respect to P?
A) Third to the right
B) Second to the right
C) Immediate left
D) Second to the left
Answer: B) Second to the right


Directions (Q6-Q10): Read the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Five girls A, B, C, D, and E are sitting in a straight row facing North.
1) C is sitting exactly between A and E.
2) D is sitting to the immediate right of E.
3) B is sitting to the immediate left of A.

Q6. Who is sitting in the middle of the row?
A) A
B) B
C) C
D) E
Answer: C) C

Q7. Who is sitting at the extreme right end of the row?
A) A
B) C
C) D
D) E
Answer: C) D

Q8. Who is sitting to the immediate left of C?
A) A
B) B
C) D
D) E
Answer: A) A

Q9. How many girls are sitting between B and D?
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 4
Answer: C) 3

Q10. What is the position of E with respect to A?
A) Immediate right
B) Second to the right
C) Immediate left
D) Second to the left
Answer: B) Second to the right


Directions (Q11-Q15): Read the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Eight persons A, B, C, D, E, F, G, and H are sitting around a circular table facing the center.
1) A is second to the left of C and third to the right of F.
2) B is exactly between A and C.
3) E is sitting opposite to A.
4) D is to the immediate right of C.
5) G is to the immediate left of H, and H is an immediate neighbor of A.

Q11. Who is sitting opposite to C?
A) F
B) G
C) H
D) E
Answer: B) G

Q12. Who is sitting third to the right of B?
A) E
B) F
C) G
D) D
Answer: A) E

Q13. Who is sitting to the immediate left of F?
A) D
B) E
C) G
D) H
Answer: B) E

Q14. How many persons are sitting between D and G when counted in a clockwise direction from D?
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 4
Answer: D) 4

Q15. What is the position of H with respect to E?
A) Second to the right
B) Third to the right
C) Third to the left
D) Immediate left
Answer: B) Third to the right


Directions (Q16-Q20): Read the following information carefully and answer the questions given below.
Six people P, Q, R, S, T, and U are sitting in two rows, three in each. Both rows are facing each other. Row 1 is facing South and Row 2 is facing North.
1) Q is not at the end of any row.
2) P is second to the left of U.
3) R, the neighbor of Q, is sitting diagonally opposite to P.
4) T is a neighbor of U.

Q16. Who is sitting opposite to Q?
A) P
B) R
C) S
D) T
Answer: D) T

Q17. Who is sitting opposite to U?
A) P
B) Q
C) R
D) S
Answer: C) R

Q18. Who among the following are sitting in the same row?
A) P and Q
B) S and R
C) R and U
D) T and S
Answer: B) S and R

Q19. Who is sitting to the immediate right of R?
A) P
B) Q
C) S
D) U
Answer: B) Q

Q20. Which of the following pairs are sitting diagonally opposite to each other, other than P and R?
A) S and U
B) Q and T
C) R and T
D) P and U
Answer: A) S and U
