Calendar Problems

Q1. What was the day of the week on 15th August 1947?
A) Thursday
B) Friday
C) Saturday
D) Sunday
Answer: B) Friday

Q2. Today is Monday. After 61 days, it will be:
A) Tuesday
B) Thursday
C) Saturday
D) Sunday
Answer: C) Saturday

Q3. If 1st January 2007 was Monday, what day of the week lies on 1st January 2008?
A) Tuesday
B) Wednesday
C) Thursday
D) Friday
Answer: A) Tuesday

Q4. How many odd days are there in 100 years?
A) 3
B) 4
C) 5
D) 6
Answer: C) 5

Q5. Which of the following is not a leap year?
A) 700
B) 800
C) 1200
D) 2000
Answer: A) 700

Q6. What was the day of the week on 28th May 2006?
A) Friday
B) Saturday
C) Sunday
D) Monday
Answer: C) Sunday

Q7. On what dates of April 2001 did Wednesday fall?
A) 1st, 8th, 15th, 22nd, 29th
B) 2nd, 9th, 16th, 23rd, 30th
C) 3rd, 10th, 17th, 24th
D) 4th, 11th, 18th, 25th
Answer: D) 4th, 11th, 18th, 25th

Q8. The last day of a century cannot be:
A) Monday
B) Wednesday
C) Tuesday
D) Friday
Answer: C) Tuesday

Q9. If 6th March 2005 is Monday, what was the day of the week on 6th March 2004?
A) Friday
B) Saturday
C) Sunday
D) Tuesday
Answer: C) Sunday

Q10. The calendar for the year 2007 will be the same for the year:
A) 2014
B) 2016
C) 2017
D) 2018
Answer: D) 2018

Q11. It was Sunday on Jan 1, 2006. What was the day of the week Jan 1, 2010?
A) Thursday
B) Friday
C) Saturday
D) Monday
Answer: B) Friday

Q12. How many days are there in x weeks x days?
A) 7x²
B) 8x
C) 14x
D) 7
Answer: B) 8x

Q13. Which of the following years has the same calendar as 1990?
A) 1996
B) 1997
C) 2000
D) 2001
Answer: D) 2001

Q14. If the 2nd of a month falls on Sunday, what day will be the 31st of that month?
A) Sunday
B) Monday
C) Tuesday
D) Wednesday
Answer: B) Monday

Q15. 15th February 2012 was Wednesday. What day of the week was 15th February 2011?
A) Monday
B) Tuesday
C) Thursday
D) Friday
Answer: B) Tuesday

Q16. The year next to 1988 having the same calendar as that of 1988 is:
A) 2012
B) 2014
C) 2016
D) 2020
Answer: C) 2016

Q17. What was the day of the week on 17th June 1998?
A) Monday
B) Tuesday
C) Wednesday
D) Thursday
Answer: C) Wednesday

Q18. If the 7th day of a month is three days earlier than Friday, what day will it be on the 19th day of the month?
A) Sunday
B) Monday
C) Wednesday
D) Friday
Answer: A) Sunday

Q19. At what interval do Leap years usually occur?
A) 2 years
B) 4 years
C) 8 years
D) 10 years
Answer: B) 4 years

Q20. If 8th December 2007 was Saturday, what day of the week was 8th December 2006?
A) Thursday
B) Friday
C) Sunday
D) Monday
Answer: B) Friday
