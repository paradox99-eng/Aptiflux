Statement & Argument

Directions (Q1-Q20): In each question below is given a statement followed by two arguments numbered I and II. You have to decide which of the arguments is a 'strong' argument and which is a 'weak' argument. 
A 'strong' argument is both important and directly related to the question. A 'weak' argument is either not directly related to the question or is of minor importance.

Q1. Statement: Should all the drugs patented and manufactured in Western countries be first tried out on a sample basis before giving a licence for sale to the general public in India?
Arguments:
I. Yes. Many such drugs require different doses and durations for the Indian population and hence it is necessary.
II. No. This is just not feasible and hence cannot be implemented.
A) Only argument I is strong
B) Only argument II is strong
C) Neither I nor II is strong
D) Both I and II are strong
Answer: A) Only argument I is strong

Q2. Statement: Should India encourage exports, when most things are insufficient for internal use itself?
Arguments:
I. Yes. We have to earn foreign exchange to pay for our imports.
II. No. Even selective encouragement would lead to shortages.
A) Only argument I is strong
B) Only argument II is strong
C) Neither I nor II is strong
D) Both I and II are strong
Answer: A) Only argument I is strong

Q3. Statement: Should there be students' unions in college/university?
Arguments:
I. No. This will create a political atmosphere on the campus and disrupt studies.
II. Yes. It is very necessary. Students are future political leaders and need this exposure.
A) Only argument I is strong
B) Only argument II is strong
C) Neither I nor II is strong
D) Both I and II are strong
Answer: D) Both I and II are strong
