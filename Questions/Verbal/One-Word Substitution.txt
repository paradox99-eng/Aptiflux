One-Word Substitution



Q1. A person who does not believe in the existence of God.
A) Theist
B) Atheist
C) Agnostic
D) Heretic
Answer: B) Atheist

Q2. A life history of a person written by himself.
A) Biography
B) History
C) Autobiography
D) Memoir
Answer: C) Autobiography

Q3. A person who looks at the bright side of things.
A) Pessimist
B) Pacifist
C) Optimist
D) Sadist
Answer: C) Optimist

Q4. A place where birds are kept.
A) Aviary
B) Apiary
C) Aquarium
D) Sanctuary
Answer: A) Aviary

Q5. A person who loves mankind.
A) Misanthrope
B) Philanthropist
C) Anthropologist
D) Mercenary
Answer: B) Philanthropist

Q6. One who is all-powerful.
A) Omniscient
B) Omnipresent
C) Omnipotent
D) Sovereign
Answer: C) Omnipotent

Q7. That which cannot be read clearly.
A) Illegible
B) Illiterate
C) Infallible
D) Inaudible
Answer: A) Illegible

Q8. A disease which spreads by physical contact.
A) Infectious
B) Contagious
C) Fatal
D) Incurable
Answer: B) Contagious

Q9. A person who knows or can speak many languages fluently.
A) Linguist
B) Polyglot
C) Bilingual
D) Translator
Answer: B) Polyglot

Q10. One who compiles a dictionary.
A) Calligrapher
B) Cartographer
C) Lexicographer
D) Choreographer
Answer: C) Lexicographer

Q11. A supposed remedy for all diseases or problems.
A) Panacea
B) Antibiotic
C) Elixir
D) Placebo
Answer: A) Panacea

Q12. A period of ten years.
A) Century
B) Millennium
C) Decade
D) Fortnight
Answer: C) Decade

Q13. One who walks in their sleep.
A) Somnambulist
B) Philatelist
C) Numismatist
D) Somniloquist
Answer: A) Somnambulist

Q14. A speech delivered without any previous preparation.
A) Monologue
B) Extempore
C) Prologue
D) Epilogue
Answer: B) Extempore

Q15. The crime of killing a king.
A) Homicide
B) Fratricide
C) Regicide
D) Suicide
Answer: C) Regicide

Q16. A person who eats human flesh.
A) Carnivore
B) Cannibal
C) Herbivore
D) Omnivore
Answer: B) Cannibal

Q17. Incapable of making mistakes or being wrong.
A) Infallible
B) Inevitable
C) Inaccessible
D) Incorrigible
Answer: A) Infallible

Q18. The study of ancient societies and their remains.
A) Anthropology
B) History
C) Archaeology
D) Sociology
Answer: C) Archaeology

Q19. A person who hates women.
A) Misanthrope
B) Misogynist
C) Philogynist
D) Feminist
Answer: B) Misogynist

Q20. A government by the people, of the people, and for the people.
A) Autocracy
B) Aristocracy
C) Democracy
D) Bureaucracy
Answer: C) Democracy
