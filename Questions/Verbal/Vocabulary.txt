Vocabulary



Q1. What is the synonym of the word "ABUNDANT"?
A) Scarce
B) Plentiful
C) Rare
D) Empty
Answer: B) Plentiful

Q2. Choose the correct meaning of the word "ELOQUENT".
A) Clumsy and awkward
B) Fluent and persuasive in speaking or writing
C) Silent and reserved
D) Confused and unclear
Answer: B) Fluent and persuasive in speaking or writing

Q3. What is the antonym of the word "OBSOLETE"?
A) Outdated
B) Contemporary
C) Ancient
D) Useless
Answer: B) Contemporary

Q4. Choose the correct word to complete the sentence: "The detective was able to _______ the truth from the suspect after hours of questioning."
A) Elicit
B) Illicit
C) Erase
D) Evade
Answer: A) Elicit

Q5. Which of the following words is a synonym for "METICULOUS"?
A) Careless
B) Sloppy
C) Careful and precise
D) Hasty
Answer: C) Careful and precise

Q6. Choose the antonym of the word "BENEVOLENT".
A) Kind
B) Generous
C) Malevolent
D) Sympathetic
Answer: C) Malevolent

Q7. Fill in the blank: "Despite the heavy rain, the football match was not _______."
A) Postponed
B) Promoted
C) Predicted
D) Prepared
Answer: A) Postponed

Q8. What does the word "UBIQUITOUS" mean?
A) Rare and hard to find
B) Present, appearing, or found everywhere
C) Extremely expensive
D) Harmful or toxic
Answer: B) Present, appearing, or found everywhere

Q9. Which word is the exact opposite of "EPHEMERAL"?
A) Temporary
B) Short-lived
C) Permanent
D) Fleeting
Answer: C) Permanent

Q10. Choose the correct word to complete the sentence: "The manager gave his _______ to the new project proposal."
A) Assent
B) Ascent
C) Accent
D) Assert
Answer: A) Assent

Q11. What is the synonym of the word "CANDID"?
A) Deceptive
B) Frank and honest
C) Secretive
D) Complicated
Answer: B) Frank and honest

Q12. Choose the exact opposite of the word "MITIGATE".
A) Alleviate
B) Reduce
C) Aggravate
D) Soothe
Answer: C) Aggravate

Q13. The word "LUCID" most nearly means:
A) Cloudy
B) Clear and easy to understand
C) Difficult
D) Mysterious
Answer: B) Clear and easy to understand

Q14. Fill in the blank: "His actions were completely _______ with his earlier promises."
A) Inconsistent
B) Incompetent
C) Inconclusive
D) Inconvenient
Answer: A) Inconsistent

Q15. Which word means "a person who is hostile or indifferent to culture and the arts"?
A) Philanthropist
B) Philistine
C) Philosopher
D) Prodigy
Answer: B) Philistine

Q16. What is the synonym of the word "LETHARGIC"?
A) Energetic
B) Sluggish
C) Active
D) Enthusiastic
Answer: B) Sluggish

Q17. Fill in the blank: "The company decided to _______ the old policy with a more flexible one."
A) Supersede
B) Surrender
C) Suppress
D) Surround
Answer: A) Supersede

Q18. What is the meaning of the word "PRAGMATIC"?
A) Idealistic and dreamy
B) Practical and realistic
C) Highly emotional
D) Purely theoretical
Answer: B) Practical and realistic

Q19. Select the antonym for the word "VINDICATE".
A) Clear
B) Absolve
C) Condemn
D) Justify
Answer: C) Condemn

Q20. Choose the correct word: "The politician delivered a highly _______ speech that angered the opposition."
A) Provocative
B) Placid
C) Pacific
D) Profound
Answer: A) Provocative
