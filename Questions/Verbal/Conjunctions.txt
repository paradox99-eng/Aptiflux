Conjunctions



Q1. He is poor _____ he is honest.
A) and
B) but
C) or
D) so
Answer: B) but

Q2. You will not succeed _____ you work hard.
A) unless
B) if
C) until
D) although
Answer: A) unless

Q3. Hardly had I reached the station _____ the train left.
A) then
B) than
C) when
D) before
Answer: C) when

Q4. No sooner did the bell ring _____ the students rushed out of the classroom.
A) then
B) than
C) when
D) but
Answer: B) than

Q5. Wait here _____ I return from the market.
A) unless
B) until
C) if
D) although
Answer: B) until

Q6. He is both a singer _____ a dancer.
A) as well as
B) and
C) or
D) also
Answer: B) and

Q7. Not only did he help me with my work, _____ he also gave me some money.
A) but
B) and
C) or
D) yet
Answer: A) but

Q8. Though he is old, _____ he is physically very strong.
A) but
B) yet
C) still
D) and
Answer: B) yet

Q9. _____ it was raining heavily, we went out for a walk.
A) Although
B) Because
C) Since
D) As
Answer: A) Although

Q10. Make hay _____ the sun shines.
A) when
B) while
C) as
D) until
Answer: B) while

Q11. I don't know _____ he will come to the party or not.
A) if
B) whether
C) that
D) when
Answer: B) whether

Q12. Walk carefully _____ you should fall.
A) unless
B) otherwise
C) lest
D) or
Answer: C) lest

Q13. She is as smart _____ her elder sister.
A) like
B) as
C) than
D) so
Answer: B) as

Q14. He worked very hard _____ he might pass the examination.
A) so that
B) because
C) since
D) as
Answer: A) so that

Q15. You can have _____ tea or coffee.
A) neither
B) either
C) both
D) whether
Answer: B) either

Q16. _____ he was ill, he did not go to school today.
A) As
B) Although
C) But
D) Unless
Answer: A) As

Q17. Neither the teacher _____ the students were present in the hall.
A) or
B) nor
C) and
D) but
Answer: B) nor

Q18. The box was so heavy _____ I could not lift it by myself.
A) that
B) as
C) than
D) because
Answer: A) that

Q19. I will call you _____ I reach home safely.
A) till
B) as soon as
C) until
D) unless
Answer: B) as soon as

Q20. He acts _____ he were the boss of this company.
A) as if
B) even though
C) although
D) like
Answer: A) as if
