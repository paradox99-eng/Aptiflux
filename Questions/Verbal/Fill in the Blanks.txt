Fill in the Blanks



Q1. I have been living in this city _____ 2010.
A) for
B) since
C) from
D) until
Answer: B) since

Q2. By the time we reached the station, the train _____ already left.
A) has
B) was
C) had
D) is
Answer: C) had

Q3. Neither the manager nor the employees _____ aware of the new policy.
A) is
B) was
C) are
D) has
Answer: C) are

Q4. He is _____ honest man who always speaks the truth.
A) a
B) an
C) the
D) no article
Answer: B) an

Q5. _____ he was tired, he stayed up late to finish his assignment.
A) Because
B) Therefore
C) Although
D) However
Answer: C) Although

Q6. The committee decided to _____ the meeting until next week.
A) dismiss
B) adjourn
C) reject
D) dissolve
Answer: B) adjourn

Q7. Please make sure to _____ the lights before leaving the room.
A) turn up
B) turn on
C) turn out
D) turn off
Answer: D) turn off

Q8. She is very good _____ playing the piano.
A) in
B) on
C) at
D) with
Answer: C) at

Q9. If I had known you were coming, I _____ baked a cake.
A) will have
B) would have
C) had
D) have
Answer: B) would have

Q10. Each of the boys gave _____ best performance during the annual sports day.
A) their
B) his
C) its
D) theirs
Answer: B) his

Q11. The new software update will _____ the overall performance of your computer.
A) degrade
B) diminish
C) enhance
D) recede
Answer: C) enhance

Q12. He speaks English so _____ that everyone thinks he is a native speaker.
A) fluent
B) fluently
C) fluency
D) more fluent
Answer: B) fluently

Q13. The cat jumped _____ the fence and ran into the woods.
A) above
B) under
C) over
D) into
Answer: C) over

Q14. You _____ wear a helmet while riding a motorcycle; it's the law.
A) might
B) may
C) must
D) could
Answer: C) must

Q15. I can't _____ what he is trying to say because of the loud noise in the background.
A) make up
B) make out
C) make off
D) make for
Answer: B) make out

Q16. I will not go to the party _____ you come with me.
A) if
B) unless
C) until
D) whereas
Answer: B) unless

Q17. She is looking forward to _____ her grandparents during the holidays.
A) visit
B) visiting
C) visited
D) be visiting
Answer: B) visiting

Q18. The teacher gave us a lot of _____ on how to prepare for the final exam.
A) advices
B) advise
C) advises
D) advice
Answer: D) advice

Q19. He was accused _____ stealing the confidential documents from the office.
A) for
B) of
C) with
D) about
Answer: B) of

Q20. Despite numerous obstacles, she remained _____ in her pursuit of the championship.
A) reluctant
B) negligent
C) steadfast
D) hostile
Answer: C) steadfast
