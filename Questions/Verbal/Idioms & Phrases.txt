Idioms & Phrases



Q1. What is the meaning of the idiom "To bite the dust"?
A) To clean thoroughly
B) To fail, die, or be defeated
C) To eat in a hurry
D) To get angry
Answer: B) To fail, die, or be defeated

Q2. What is the meaning of the phrase "A blessing in disguise"?
A) A good thing that seemed bad at first
B) A prayer offered in secret
C) A person who hides their true intentions
D) A gift given anonymously
Answer: A) A good thing that seemed bad at first

Q3. What does the idiom "Spill the beans" mean?
A) To drop groceries
B) To reveal a secret
C) To cook a meal
D) To make a mess
Answer: B) To reveal a secret

Q4. What is the meaning of "Beat around the bush"?
A) To search for something thoroughly
B) To avoid getting to the main point
C) To trim the garden
D) To run in circles
Answer: B) To avoid getting to the main point

Q5. What does it mean to "Burn the midnight oil"?
A) To light a fire at night
B) To waste resources
C) To work or study late into the night
D) To sleep late
Answer: C) To work or study late into the night

Q6. What is the meaning of the idiom "A piece of cake"?
A) A sweet reward
B) Something that is very easy to do
C) A small portion
D) A celebration
Answer: B) Something that is very easy to do

Q7. What does the phrase "Cost an arm and a leg" mean?
A) To require physical labor
B) To be physically disabled
C) To be very expensive
D) To demand a sacrifice
Answer: C) To be very expensive

Q8. What is the meaning of "Hit the nail on the head"?
A) To do or say something exactly right
B) To repair something broken
C) To hurt someone accidentally
D) To guess randomly
Answer: A) To do or say something exactly right

Q9. What does the idiom "Once in a blue moon" refer to?
A) Something that happens frequently
B) Something that happens very rarely
C) A romantic evening
D) A lunar eclipse
Answer: B) Something that happens very rarely

Q10. What is the meaning of "Let the cat out of the bag"?
A) To free an animal
B) To lose a pet
C) To accidentally reveal a secret
D) To make a mistake
Answer: C) To accidentally reveal a secret

Q11. What does it mean to "Break the ice"?
A) To do something dangerous
B) To shatter a window
C) To initiate conversation in a tense or formal setting
D) To drink a cold beverage
Answer: C) To initiate conversation in a tense or formal setting

Q12. What is the meaning of the idiom "Under the weather"?
A) Caught in the rain
B) Feeling ill or sick
C) Depressed about the climate
D) Facing a storm
Answer: B) Feeling ill or sick

Q13. What does "Barking up the wrong tree" mean?
A) Climbing a tree
B) Yelling at a dog
C) Pursuing a false lead or accusing the wrong person
D) Making a loud noise
Answer: C) Pursuing a false lead or accusing the wrong person

Q14. What is the meaning of "Turn a blind eye"?
A) To lose one's vision
B) To look the other way and ignore something wrong
C) To be confused
D) To sleep deeply
Answer: B) To look the other way and ignore something wrong

Q15. What does it mean to "Bite the bullet"?
A) To fire a gun
B) To eat something hard
C) To endure a painful or difficult situation bravely
D) To dodge a problem
Answer: C) To endure a painful or difficult situation bravely

Q16. What is the meaning of "Jump on the bandwagon"?
A) To play an instrument
B) To join a popular trend or activity
C) To travel with a group
D) To leap over a hurdle
Answer: B) To join a popular trend or activity

Q17. What does the phrase "To add insult to injury" mean?
A) To verbally abuse someone
B) To make a bad situation even worse
C) To physically harm someone
D) To apologize for a mistake
Answer: B) To make a bad situation even worse

Q18. What is the meaning of "Through thick and thin"?
A) Through dense forests
B) Losing and gaining weight
C) Under all circumstances, no matter how difficult
D) Traveling long distances
Answer: C) Under all circumstances, no matter how difficult

Q19. What does it mean to "Sit on the fence"?
A) To watch a game
B) To build a barrier
C) To remain neutral or undecided
D) To rest outside
Answer: C) To remain neutral or undecided

Q20. What is the meaning of a "Wild goose chase"?
A) A foolish and hopeless search or pursuit
B) A successful hunting trip
C) A fast race
D) An animal rescue
Answer: A) A foolish and hopeless search or pursuit
