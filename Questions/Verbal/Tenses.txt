Tenses



Q1. I _____ on this project for three months.
A) am working
B) have been working
C) was working
D) had been working
Answer: B) have been working

Q2. By the time we reached the station, the train _____.
A) left
B) was leaving
C) had left
D) has left
Answer: C) had left

Q3. She usually _____ to the office by bus.
A) go
B) going
C) goes
D) went
Answer: C) goes

Q4. I _____ my homework before my mother comes back from work.
A) will finish
B) will have finished
C) finish
D) have finished
Answer: B) will have finished

Q5. We _____ to London next week. The tickets are already booked.
A) are flying
B) fly
C) flew
D) have flown
Answer: A) are flying

Q6. He _____ a novel when the phone rang.
A) reads
B) is reading
C) was reading
D) had read
Answer: C) was reading

Q7. Water _____ at 100 degrees Celsius.
A) boil
B) boils
C) is boiling
D) has boiled
Answer: B) boils

Q8. They _____ each other since childhood.
A) know
B) are knowing
C) have known
D) have been knowing
Answer: C) have known

Q9. If it rains tomorrow, we _____ the match.
A) cancel
B) cancelled
C) will cancel
D) would cancel
Answer: C) will cancel

Q10. I _____ the Taj Mahal last year.
A) visit
B) visited
C) have visited
D) had visited
Answer: B) visited

Q11. Look at those dark clouds! It _____.
A) will rain
B) is going to rain
C) rains
D) rained
Answer: B) is going to rain

Q12. He _____ in this multinational company since 2015.
A) worked
B) works
C) is working
D) has been working
Answer: D) has been working

Q13. While I was studying, my brother _____ TV.
A) watched
B) was watching
C) watches
D) has watched
Answer: B) was watching

Q14. I wish I _____ a millionaire so I could travel the world.
A) am
B) was
C) were
D) have been
Answer: C) were

Q15. She _____ for the bus when the accident happened across the street.
A) waited
B) was waiting
C) had waited
D) has been waiting
Answer: B) was waiting

Q16. The sun _____ in the east and sets in the west.
A) rise
B) rises
C) is rising
D) rose
Answer: B) rises

Q17. After he _____ his lunch, he went out to play with his friends.
A) finishes
B) finished
C) had finished
D) has finished
Answer: C) had finished

Q18. It is high time you _____ studying seriously for the final exams.
A) start
B) started
C) should start
D) are starting
Answer: B) started

Q19. I _____ the new superhero movie yet.
A) didn't see
B) haven't seen
C) hadn't seen
D) don't see
Answer: B) haven't seen

Q20. By the end of next year, she _____ her graduation.
A) completes
B) will complete
C) will have completed
D) has completed
Answer: C) will have completed
