Sentence Completion



Q1. The new employee proved to be highly _____, completing all her assignments well before the deadlines.
A) lethargic
B) efficient
C) careless
D) hesitant
Answer: B) efficient

Q2. Despite the intense heat and exhaustion, the marathon runner _____ and successfully crossed the finish line.
A) surrendered
B) retreated
C) persevered
D) collapsed
Answer: C) persevered

Q3. The professor's lecture was so _____ that many students found themselves nodding off to sleep.
A) captivating
B) exhilarating
C) monotonous
D) informative
Answer: C) monotonous

Q4. Because the evidence against the suspect was _____, the jury had no choice but to acquit him.
A) overwhelming
B) conclusive
C) undeniable
D) inconclusive
Answer: D) inconclusive

Q5. The company decided to _____ its operations overseas in order to reach a wider global market.
A) abandon
B) expand
C) shrink
D) conceal
Answer: B) expand

Q6. Her _____ attitude toward the project frustrated her team members, who were working late every night to succeed.
A) passionate
B) dedicated
C) apathetic
D) enthusiastic
Answer: C) apathetic

Q7. The sudden thunderstorm forced the organizers to _____ the outdoor concert until the following weekend.
A) cancel
B) prepone
C) postpone
D) promote
Answer: C) postpone

Q8. The medication helped to _____ the patient's pain, allowing him to sleep peacefully through the night.
A) aggravate
B) elevate
C) alleviate
D) agitate
Answer: C) alleviate

Q9. As a skilled negotiator, she was able to resolve the _____ dispute between the two rival companies.
A) harmonious
B) amicable
C) contentious
D) peaceful
Answer: C) contentious

Q10. The architect designed the building with a _____ structure to withstand severe earthquakes.
A) fragile
B) robust
C) delicate
D) brittle
Answer: B) robust

Q11. Due to the heavy fog, the pilot's visibility was completely _____, making the landing extremely dangerous.
A) enhanced
B) illuminated
C) obscured
D) clarified
Answer: C) obscured

Q12. The child was completely _____ in the fascinating book, ignoring the loud noises coming from the street outside.
A) distracted
B) engrossed
C) confused
D) terrified
Answer: B) engrossed

Q13. The manager gave a _____ explanation of the new policy, ensuring that everyone understood it without any confusion.
A) vague
B) ambiguous
C) comprehensive
D) confusing
Answer: C) comprehensive

Q14. His _____ spending habits eventually led to his financial ruin and bankruptcy.
A) thrifty
B) extravagant
C) economical
D) moderate
Answer: B) extravagant

Q15. The witness's testimony was so _____ that the lawyer had no trouble poking holes in her story.
A) inconsistent
B) irrefutable
C) flawless
D) convincing
Answer: A) inconsistent

Q16. After working tirelessly for weeks without a break, she was completely _____ and needed a long vacation.
A) energized
B) exhausted
C) invincible
D) refreshed
Answer: B) exhausted

Q17. The chef added a secret blend of spices to the soup to _____ its flavor.
A) diminish
B) mask
C) enhance
D) ruin
Answer: C) enhance

Q18. The old bridge is structurally unsound; therefore, it is _____ that we repair it immediately.
A) optional
B) trivial
C) imperative
D) irrelevant
Answer: C) imperative

Q19. Despite his immense wealth, he lived a remarkably _____ lifestyle, avoiding any unnecessary luxuries.
A) lavish
B) frugal
C) ostentatious
D) extravagant
Answer: B) frugal

Q20. The detective's _____ attention to detail allowed him to spot the tiny clue that everyone else had missed.
A) careless
B) hasty
C) superficial
D) meticulous
