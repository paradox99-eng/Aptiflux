National Current Affairs



Q1. India successfully landed the Chandrayaan-3 spacecraft on the Moon in 2023. Near which region of the moon did it land?
A) Lunar Equator
B) Lunar North Pole
C) Lunar South Pole
D) Far side of the Moon
Answer: C) Lunar South Pole

Q2. What is the official name given to the landing site of the Chandrayaan-3 lander on the moon?
A) Tiranga Point
B) Shiv Shakti Point
C) Bharat Point
D) Kalam Point
Answer: B) Shiv Shakti Point

Q3. India hosted the G20 Leaders' Summit for the first time in which year?
A) 2021
B) 2022
C) 2023
D) 2024
Answer: C) 2023

Q4. What was the theme of the G20 Summit hosted by India in New Delhi?
A) Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam (One Earth, One Family, One Future)
B) Sabka Saath Sabka Vikas (Together with All, Development for All)
C) Atmanirbhar Bharat (Self-Reliant India)
D) Global Unity for a Sustainable Future
Answer: A) Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam (One Earth, One Family, One Future)

Q5. Which new permanent member was inducted into the G20 during the summit hosted by India?
A) African Union
B) Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN)
C) South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation (SAARC)
D) Arab League
Answer: A) African Union

Q6. The new Parliament building of India, inaugurated in 2023, was designed by which prominent architect?
A) Raj Rewal
B) Bimal Patel
C) Charles Correa
D) Hafeez Contractor
Answer: B) Bimal Patel

Q7. What is the name of the historic golden sceptre installed near the Speaker's chair in the new Parliament building?
A) Danda
B) Sengol
C) Trishul
D) Chakra
Answer: B) Sengol

Q8. The Women's Reservation Bill passed by the Indian Parliament provides what percentage of reservation for women in the Lok Sabha and State Assemblies?
A) 25%
B) 30%
C) 33%
D) 50%
Answer: C) 33%

Q9. What is the official Hindi name of the Women's Reservation Act passed by the Parliament?
A) Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam
B) Mahila Samman Act
C) Nari Suraksha Sanhita
D) Matritva Vandana Adhiniyam
Answer: A) Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam

Q10. India's first dedicated solar space mission, launched by ISRO to study the Sun, is named:
A) Surya-L1
B) Aditya-L1
C) Bhaskar-1
D) Ravi-L1
Answer: B) Aditya-L1

Q11. Who is the current Chief Justice of India (as appointed in late 2022 and serving through 2023-2024)?
A) Justice U. U. Lalit
B) Justice N. V. Ramana
C) Justice D. Y. Chandrachud
D) Justice Ranjan Gogoi
Answer: C) Justice D. Y. Chandrachud

Q12. The 'PM Vishwakarma' scheme was launched by the Central Government primarily to support which group of people?
A) Marginal farmers
B) IT Professionals and Startups
C) Traditional artisans and craftspeople
D) Medical practitioners in rural areas
Answer: C) Traditional artisans and craftspeople

Q13. India's longest sea bridge, the Mumbai Trans Harbour Link (MTHL) inaugurated in Maharashtra, is named after:
A) Mahatma Gandhi
B) Atal Bihari Vajpayee
C) Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj
D) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
Answer: B) Atal Bihari Vajpayee

Q14. The new criminal laws passed by the Indian Parliament to replace the IPC, CrPC, and Indian Evidence Act are collectively named:
A) Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam
B) Rashtriya Nyaya Sanhita, Rashtriya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, Rashtriya Sakshya Adhiniyam
C) Bharat Kanoon Sanhita, Bharat Suraksha Sanhita, Bharat Nyaya Adhiniyam
D) Bharatiya Penal Code, Bharatiya Criminal Code, Bharatiya Evidence Act
Answer: A) Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam

Q15. Which two cities were jointly awarded the title of the cleanest city in India in the Swachh Survekshan Awards 2023?
A) Indore and Surat
B) Indore and Bhopal
C) Surat and Mysuru
D) Bhopal and Pune
Answer: A) Indore and Surat

Q16. The ambitious 'Gaganyaan' mission of ISRO aims to:
A) Send a robotic rover to Mars
B) Send humans to space and bring them back safely
C) Explore the depths of the Indian Ocean
D) Establish a permanent space station on the Moon
Answer: B) Send humans to space and bring them back safely

Q17. What is the name of the International Exhibition-cum-Convention Centre (IECC) complex at Pragati Maidan, New Delhi, where the G20 summit was held?
A) Yashobhoomi
B) Bharat Mandapam
C) Vigyan Bhavan

Q18. The 'Mera Yuva Bharat' (MY Bharat) autonomous body was launched by the Prime Minister to benefit:

Q19. Which state in India became the first to pass the Uniform Civil Code (UCC) Bill in its legislative assembly in early 2024?
