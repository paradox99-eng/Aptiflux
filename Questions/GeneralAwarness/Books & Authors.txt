Books & Authors



Q1. Who is the author of the famous historical book 'The Discovery of India'?
A) Mahatma Gandhi
B) Jawaharlal Nehru
C) Subhas Chandra Bose
D) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
Answer: B) Jawaharlal Nehru

Q2. Who wrote the Indian national anthem 'Jana Gana Mana'?
A) Bankim Chandra Chatterjee
B) Rabindranath Tagore
C) Sarojini Naidu
D) Sri Aurobindo
Answer: B) Rabindranath Tagore

Q3. The great Indian epic 'Mahabharata' is traditionally attributed to which sage?
A) Valmiki
B) Tulsidas
C) Ved Vyasa
D) Kalidasa
Answer: C) Ved Vyasa

Q4. Who is the author of the world-famous 'Harry Potter' fantasy book series?
A) J.R.R. Tolkien
B) George R.R. Martin
C) J.K. Rowling
D) Stephen King
Answer: C) J.K. Rowling

Q5. Which famous Indian author wrote the Sahitya Akademi Award-winning novel 'The Guide'?
A) Mulk Raj Anand
B) R.K. Narayan
C) Ruskin Bond
D) Khushwant Singh
Answer: B) R.K. Narayan

Q6. 'Wings of Fire' is the highly inspiring autobiography of which prominent Indian personality?
A) Homi J. Bhabha
B) Vikram Sarabhai
C) A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
D) C.V. Raman
Answer: C) A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Q7. Who wrote the famous dystopian novel '1984'?
A) George Orwell
B) Aldous Huxley
C) Ray Bradbury
D) H.G. Wells
Answer: A) George Orwell

Q8. The renowned book 'The Story of My Experiments with Truth' is the autobiography of:
A) Jawaharlal Nehru
B) Bhagat Singh
C) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
D) Mahatma Gandhi
Answer: D) Mahatma Gandhi

Q9. Who is the author of 'Gitanjali', a collection of poems for which he won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913?
A) Munshi Premchand
B) Rabindranath Tagore
C) Bankim Chandra Chatterjee
D) R.K. Narayan
Answer: B) Rabindranath Tagore

Q10. 'Midnight's Children', which won the Booker Prize in 1981, is a famous novel written by:
A) Amitav Ghosh
B) Salman Rushdie
C) V.S. Naipaul
D) Jhumpa Lahiri
Answer: B) Salman Rushdie

Q11. Who wrote the classic English literary novel 'Pride and Prejudice'?
A) Emily Brontë
B) Charlotte Brontë
C) Jane Austen
D) Mary Shelley
Answer: C) Jane Austen

Q12. The ancient Indian text on statecraft, economic policy, and military strategy, 'Arthashastra', was authored by:
A) Aryabhata
B) Chanakya (Kautilya)
C) Banabhatta
D) Ashvaghosha
Answer: B) Chanakya (Kautilya)

Q13. Who is the author of the best-selling novel 'The God of Small Things', which won the Booker Prize in 1997?
A) Kiran Desai
B) Arundhati Roy
C) Chetan Bhagat
D) Anita Desai
Answer: B) Arundhati Roy

Q14. 'A Suitable Boy', one of the longest novels ever published in a single volume in the English language, is written by:
A) Vikram Seth
B) Aravind Adiga
C) Shashi Tharoor
D) Salman Rushdie
Answer: A) Vikram Seth

Q15. Who wrote the famous tragic play 'Macbeth'?
A) Charles Dickens
B) William Shakespeare
C) Christopher Marlowe
D) John Milton
Answer: B) William Shakespeare

Q16. The widely read book 'Ignited Minds: Unleashing the Power Within India' is authored by:
A) Amartya Sen
B) A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
C) Raghuram Rajan
D) Shashi Tharoor
Answer: B) A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Q17. Who is the author of the epic high-fantasy novel series 'The Lord of the Rings'?
A) C.S. Lewis
B) J.K. Rowling
C) J.R.R. Tolkien
D) Arthur Conan Doyle
Answer: C) J.R.R. Tolkien

Q18. 'Train to Pakistan', a highly acclaimed historical novel about the partition of India, was written by:
A) Mulk Raj Anand
B) R.K. Narayan
C) Khushwant Singh
D) Ruskin Bond
Answer: C) Khushwant Singh

Q19. Who wrote the ancient Sanskrit grammatical text 'Ashtadhyayi', which set the linguistic standards for Classical Sanskrit?
A) Panini
B) Patanjali
C) Charaka
D) Sushruta
Answer: A) Panini

Q20. The famous popular science book 'A Brief History of Time' was written by which renowned physicist?
A) Albert Einstein
B) Isaac Newton
C) Stephen Hawking
D) Carl Sagan
Answer: C) Stephen Hawking
