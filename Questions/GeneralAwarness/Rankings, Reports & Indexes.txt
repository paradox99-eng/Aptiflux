Rankings, Reports & Indexes



Q1. Which prominent international organization publishes the widely cited 'World Economic Outlook' report?
A) World Bank
B) International Monetary Fund (IMF)
C) World Economic Forum (WEF)
D) United Nations
Answer: B) International Monetary Fund (IMF)

Q2. The 'Global Competitiveness Report' is published annually by the:
A) World Bank
B) World Trade Organization (WTO)
C) World Economic Forum (WEF)
D) International Monetary Fund (IMF)
Answer: C) World Economic Forum (WEF)

Q3. Which organization is responsible for releasing the 'Human Development Index' (HDI)?
A) World Bank
B) United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)
C) World Health Organization (WHO)
D) UNESCO
Answer: B) United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)

Q4. The famous 'Ease of Doing Business' report (which was discontinued in 2021) was historically published by which institution?
A) World Bank
B) World Economic Forum (WEF)
C) International Monetary Fund (IMF)
D) World Trade Organization (WTO)
Answer: A) World Bank

Q5. Which organization is responsible for publishing the 'Global Innovation Index' (GII)?
A) World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO)
B) World Economic Forum (WEF)
C) United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP)
D) World Trade Organization (WTO)
Answer: A) World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO)

Q6. The 'World Press Freedom Index' is compiled and published annually by:
A) Amnesty International
B) Reporters Without Borders (RSF)
C) Human Rights Watch
D) United Nations
Answer: B) Reporters Without Borders (RSF)

Q7. Which of the following parameters is NOT used to calculate the Human Development Index (HDI)?
A) Life expectancy at birth
B) Expected years of schooling
C) Gross National Income (GNI) per capita
D) Carbon footprint per capita
Answer: D) Carbon footprint per capita

Q8. The 'Global Gender Gap Report' is published by which international body?
A) UN Women
B) World Economic Forum (WEF)
C) World Bank
D) International Labour Organization (ILO)
Answer: B) World Economic Forum (WEF)

Q9. Which institution consistently publishes the 'World Happiness Report'?
A) United Nations Sustainable Development Solutions Network (UN SDSN)
B) World Health Organization (WHO)
C) Amnesty International
D) Oxford University
Answer: A) United Nations Sustainable Development Solutions Network (UN SDSN)

Q10. The 'Global Hunger Index' (GHI) is jointly published by Concern Worldwide and:
A) Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO)
B) Welthungerhilfe
C) World Food Programme (WFP)
D) UNICEF
Answer: B) Welthungerhilfe

Q11. The 'Corruption Perceptions Index' (CPI), which ranks countries by their perceived levels of public sector corruption, is released by:
A) Transparency International
B) World Bank
C) Interpol
D) UN Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC)
Answer: A) Transparency International

Q12. Which organization releases the 'Global Financial Stability Report'?
A) World Bank
B) International Monetary Fund (IMF)
C) Financial Action Task Force (FATF)
D) Bank for International Settlements (BIS)
Answer: B) International Monetary Fund (IMF)

Q13. The 'Living Planet Report', which tracks global biodiversity and the health of the planet, is published by:
A) United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP)
B) World Wide Fund for Nature (WWF)
C) Greenpeace
D) International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN)
Answer: B) World Wide Fund for Nature (WWF)

Q14. Which body publishes the 'World Investment Report' annually?
A) UNCTAD (United Nations Conference on Trade and Development)
B) World Bank
C) International Monetary Fund (IMF)
D) World Trade Organization (WTO)
Answer: A) UNCTAD (United Nations Conference on Trade and Development)

Q15. The 'Global Peace Index' (GPI) is produced annually by the:
A) United Nations Security Council
B) Amnesty International
C) Institute for Economics and Peace (IEP)
D) Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI)
Answer: C) Institute for Economics and Peace (IEP)

Q16. The 'State of the World's Children' is a flagship annual report published by:
A) Save the Children
B) UNICEF
C) World Health Organization (WHO)
D) UNESCO
Answer: B) UNICEF

Q17. Which organization publishes the annual 'Global Risks Report' ahead of its prestigious annual meeting?
A) World Bank
B) International Monetary Fund (IMF)
C) World Economic Forum (WEF)
D) United Nations
Answer: C) World Economic Forum (WEF)

Q18. The 'Multidimensional Poverty Index' (MPI) is published by the UNDP in collaboration with:
A) World Bank
B) Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (OPHI)
C) Harvard University
D) London School of Economics
Answer: B) Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (OPHI)

Q19. Which organization releases the 'Energy Transition Index' (ETI)?
A) International Energy Agency (IEA)
B) World Economic Forum (WEF)
C) Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (OPEC)
D) United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP)
Answer: B) World Economic Forum (WEF)

Q20. The 'World Development Report' (WDR) is an annual flagship publication of the:
A) United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)
B) World Bank
C) International Monetary Fund (IMF)
D) World Trade Organization (WTO)
Answer: B) World Bank
