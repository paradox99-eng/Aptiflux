International Organizations



Q1. Where is the headquarters of the United Nations (UN) located?
A) Geneva
B) New York
C) Paris
D) Vienna
Answer: B) New York

Q2. When was the United Nations officially established?
A) October 24, 1945
B) January 1, 1946
C) August 15, 1947
D) December 10, 1948
Answer: A) October 24, 1945

Q3. The International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the World Bank were created in 1944 and are often referred to as the:
A) Bretton Woods Twins
B) Geneva Sisters
C) Washington Alliance
D) Atlantic Partners
Answer: A) Bretton Woods Twins

Q4. Where is the headquarters of the World Health Organization (WHO) located?
A) New York, USA
B) Washington D.C., USA
C) Geneva, Switzerland
D) Rome, Italy
Answer: C) Geneva, Switzerland

Q5. How many permanent member countries are there in the United Nations Security Council (UNSC)?
A) 5
B) 10
C) 15
D) 20
Answer: A) 5

Q6. Which of the following is NOT a permanent member of the UN Security Council?
A) China
B) France
C) India
D) United Kingdom
Answer: C) India

Q7. Where is the headquarters of the World Trade Organization (WTO)?
A) Geneva, Switzerland
B) Paris, France
C) New York, USA
D) London, UK
Answer: A) Geneva, Switzerland

Q8. The World Trade Organization (WTO) replaced which of the following organizations in 1995?
A) UNICEF
B) GATT (General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade)
C) UNCTAD
D) ILO
Answer: B) GATT (General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade)

Q9. UNESCO stands for United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization. Where is its headquarters?
A) Rome
B) Paris
C) Geneva
D) Vienna
Answer: B) Paris

Q10. Which prominent international organization publishes the 'World Economic Outlook' report?
A) World Bank
B) World Economic Forum (WEF)
C) International Monetary Fund (IMF)
D) World Trade Organization (WTO)
Answer: C) International Monetary Fund (IMF)

Q11. The headquarters of the International Court of Justice (ICJ) is located at:
A) The Hague, Netherlands
B) Geneva, Switzerland
C) New York, USA
D) Vienna, Austria
Answer: A) The Hague, Netherlands

Q12. SAARC (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation) was established in which year?
A) 1980
B) 1985
C) 1990
D) 1995
Answer: B) 1985

Q13. The secretariat (headquarters) of SAARC is located in:
A) New Delhi, India
B) Dhaka, Bangladesh
C) Kathmandu, Nepal
D) Colombo, Sri Lanka
Answer: C) Kathmandu, Nepal

Q14. NATO (North Atlantic Treaty Organization) is primarily a:
A) Global Economic Alliance
B) Military Alliance
C) Cultural Organization
D) Trade Union
Answer: B) Military Alliance

Q15. Where is the headquarters of the International Labour Organization (ILO) located?
A) Geneva
B) Paris
C) Washington D.C.
D) Rome
Answer: A) Geneva

Q16. The Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) of the United Nations has its headquarters in:
A) Paris, France
B) Rome, Italy
C) New York, USA
D) Geneva, Switzerland
Answer: B) Rome, Italy

Q17. Which of the following countries is NOT a member of the BRICS group?
A) Brazil
B) Russia
C) India
D) Singapore
Answer: D) Singapore

Q18. The 'New Development Bank' (NDB), formerly referred to as the BRICS Development Bank, is headquartered in:
A) Moscow, Russia
B) New Delhi, India
C) Shanghai, China
D) Beijing, China
Answer: C) Shanghai, China

Q19. Where is the headquarters of the Asian Development Bank (ADB)?
A) Manila, Philippines
B) Tokyo, Japan
C) Beijing, China
D) Jakarta, Indonesia
Answer: A) Manila, Philippines

Q20. ASEAN stands for Association of Southeast Asian Nations. Its secretariat is located in:
A) Bangkok, Thailand
B) Singapore
C) Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
D) Jakarta, Indonesia
Answer: D) Jakarta, Indonesia
