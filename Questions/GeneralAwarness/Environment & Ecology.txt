Environment & Ecology



Q1. The 'Greenhouse Effect' is primarily caused by an increase in the atmospheric concentration of which gas?
A) Oxygen
B) Nitrogen
C) Carbon Dioxide
D) Argon
Answer: C) Carbon Dioxide

Q2. Which layer of the Earth's atmosphere contains the ozone layer that protects us from harmful ultraviolet (UV) radiation?
A) Troposphere
B) Stratosphere
C) Mesosphere
D) Thermosphere
Answer: B) Stratosphere

Q3. World Environment Day is celebrated globally every year on:
A) April 22
B) May 22
C) June 5
D) July 11
Answer: C) June 5

Q4. The term 'Biodiversity' fundamentally refers to:
A) The variety of life forms on Earth at all levels
B) The study of non-living components of an ecosystem
C) The process of commercial forestation
D) The exclusive study of global warming patterns
Answer: A) The variety of life forms on Earth at all levels

Q5. Which of the following is an example of a renewable source of energy?
A) Coal
B) Natural Gas
C) Solar Energy
D) Petroleum
Answer: C) Solar Energy

Q6. The 'Kyoto Protocol' is an international treaty primarily aimed at reducing:
A) Plastic pollution in oceans
B) Greenhouse gas emissions
C) Nuclear weapon testing
D) Deforestation rates
Answer: B) Greenhouse gas emissions

Q7. Which of the following air pollutants are considered the main cause of 'Acid Rain'?
A) Carbon Monoxide and Ozone
B) Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)
C) Sulfur Dioxide and Nitrogen Oxides
D) Methane and Argon
Answer: C) Sulfur Dioxide and Nitrogen Oxides

Q8. The 'Red List' (or Red Data Book), which contains a comprehensive record of endangered species worldwide, is published by:
A) WWF (World Wide Fund for Nature)
B) IUCN (International Union for Conservation of Nature)
C) UNEP (United Nations Environment Programme)
D) WHO (World Health Organization)
Answer: B) IUCN (International Union for Conservation of Nature)

Q9. Which of the following is NOT a greenhouse gas?
A) Methane
B) Carbon Dioxide
C) Water Vapor
D) Nitrogen
Answer: D) Nitrogen

Q10. 'BOD' stands for Biochemical Oxygen Demand. It is a standard criterion for measuring the level of:
A) Air pollution
B) Soil pollution
C) Water pollution (organic load)
D) Noise pollution
Answer: C) Water pollution (organic load)

Q11. The depletion of the ozone layer is predominantly caused by the release of:
A) Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)
B) Carbon Dioxide
C) Methane
D) Sulfur Dioxide
Answer: A) Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)

Q12. The famous 'Chipko Movement' originating in India was aimed at:
A) Preventing river water pollution
B) Protecting trees from being cut down by hugging them
C) Promoting wildlife hunting for indigenous tribes
D) Banning the use of single-use plastic bags
Answer: B) Protecting trees from being cut down by hugging them

Q13. Which type of ecosystem covers the largest area of the Earth's surface?
A) Desert ecosystem
B) Forest ecosystem
C) Marine ecosystem
D) Grassland ecosystem
Answer: C) Marine ecosystem

Q14. 'Eutrophication' in a water body is primarily caused by an excessive runoff of:
A) Heavy toxic metals
B) Nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus (from fertilizers)
C) Industrial cooling water
D) Dissolved oxygen
Answer: B) Nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus (from fertilizers)

Q15. The landmark 2015 international treaty on climate change, which aims to limit global warming to well below 2 degrees Celsius, is known as the:
A) Montreal Protocol
B) Kyoto Protocol
C) Paris Agreement
D) Geneva Convention
Answer: C) Paris Agreement

Q16. An organism whose presence, absence, or abundance reflects a specific environmental condition and serves as a measure of the environmental health of an ecosystem is called an:
A) Invasive species
B) Indicator species
C) Keystone species
D) Endemic species
Answer: B) Indicator species

Q17. What is the primary purpose of 'Project Tiger', launched in India in 1973?
A) To safely breed tigers for international zoos
B) To legally promote and regulate tiger hunting
C) To ensure a viable population of Bengal tigers in their natural habitats
D) To relocate tigers to uninhabited islands
Answer: C) To ensure a viable population of Bengal tigers in their natural habitats

Q18. The 'Ramsar Convention' is a prominent international treaty established for the conservation and sustainable use of:
A) Tropical Forests
B) Wetlands
C) Deserts
D) High-altitude Mountains
Answer: B) Wetlands

Q19. Species that are found naturally in only one specific geographic area and nowhere else in the world are called:
A) Endangered species
B) Extinct species
C) Endemic species
D) Exotic species
Answer: C) Endemic species

Q20. Which of the following common waste materials is strictly non-biodegradable?
A) Vegetable peels
B) Paper products
C) Wood and timber
D) Plastic bottles
Answer: D) Plastic bottles
