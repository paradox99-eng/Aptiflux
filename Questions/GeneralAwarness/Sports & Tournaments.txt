Sports & Tournaments



Q1. The modern Summer Olympic Games are held every how many years?
A) 2 years
B) 3 years
C) 4 years
D) 5 years
Answer: C) 4 years

Q2. Which country has won the most FIFA Men's World Cup titles in football history?
A) Germany
B) Italy
C) Argentina
D) Brazil
Answer: D) Brazil

Q3. In professional tennis, which of the following Grand Slam tournaments is played on a red clay court?
A) Australian Open
B) French Open (Roland Garros)
C) Wimbledon
D) US Open
Answer: B) French Open (Roland Garros)

Q4. The term 'Grand Slam' is most commonly associated with which two sports?
A) Tennis and Golf
B) Cricket and Football
C) Baseball and Basketball
D) Boxing and Wrestling
Answer: A) Tennis and Golf

Q5. Which football player holds the record for the most goals scored in the history of the FIFA World Cup tournaments?
