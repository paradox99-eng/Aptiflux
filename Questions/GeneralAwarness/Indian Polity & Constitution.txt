Indian Polity & Constitution



Q1. Who is widely recognized as the Father of the Indian Constitution?
A) Jawaharlal Nehru
B) Dr. B. R. Ambedkar
C) Dr. Rajendra Prasad
D) Mahatma Gandhi
Answer: B) Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

Q2. On which date was the Constitution of India formally adopted by the Constituent Assembly?
A) 26th January 1950
B) 26th November 1949
C) 15th August 1947
D) 30th January 1948
Answer: B) 26th November 1949

Q3. Which group of Articles in the Indian Constitution deals with the 'Right to Equality'?
A) Articles 14 to 18
B) Articles 19 to 22
C) Articles 23 and 24
D) Articles 25 to 28
Answer: A) Articles 14 to 18

Q4. What is the minimum age required to become the President of India?
A) 25 years
B) 30 years
C) 35 years
D) 40 years
Answer: C) 35 years

Q5. Who has the constitutional power to declare a National Emergency in India?
A) The Prime Minister
B) The President
C) The Chief Justice of India
D) The Parliament
Answer: B) The President

Q6. The maximum strength of the Lok Sabha (House of the People) as prescribed in the Constitution is:
A) 545
B) 550
C) 552
D) 530
Answer: C) 552

Q7. The concept of 'Fundamental Rights' in the Indian Constitution was heavily borrowed from the Constitution of which country?
A) United Kingdom
B) USSR
C) USA
D) Ireland
Answer: C) USA

Q8. Who administers the oath of office to the President of India?
A) The Prime Minister
B) The Vice President
C) The Chief Justice of India
D) The Speaker of the Lok Sabha
Answer: C) The Chief Justice of India

Q9. Which Schedule of the Indian Constitution contains the list of recognized official languages?
A) Sixth Schedule
B) Seventh Schedule
C) Eighth Schedule
D) Ninth Schedule
Answer: C) Eighth Schedule

Q10. What is the retirement age of a Judge of the Supreme Court of India?
A) 60 years
B) 62 years
C) 65 years
D) 70 years
Answer: C) 65 years

Q11. Who serves as the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha?
A) The President
B) The Vice President
C) The Prime Minister
D) The Speaker of the Lok Sabha
Answer: B) The Vice President

Q12. How many Fundamental Duties are currently listed in the Indian Constitution under Article 51A?
A) 9
B) 10
C) 11
D) 12
Answer: C) 11

Q13. The Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP) are enumerated in which Part of the Constitution?
A) Part II
B) Part III
C) Part IV
D) Part V
Answer: C) Part IV

Q14. Under which Article can the 'President's Rule' be imposed in a State due to the failure of constitutional machinery?
A) Article 352
B) Article 356
C) Article 360
D) Article 370
Answer: B) Article 356

Q15. The Panchayati Raj system of local self-government was first adopted by which Indian state?
A) Rajasthan
B) Gujarat
C) Andhra Pradesh
D) Maharashtra
Answer: A) Rajasthan

Q16. Who appoints the Chief Election Commissioner of India?
A) The Parliament
B) The Prime Minister
C) The President
D) The Chief Justice of India
Answer: C) The President

Q17. What is the standard tenure of a member of the Rajya Sabha (Council of States)?
A) 4 years
B) 5 years
C) 6 years
D) Lifetime
Answer: C) 6 years

Q18. The 'Right to Constitutional Remedies', which Dr. B. R. Ambedkar called the 'heart and soul of the Constitution', is guaranteed under:
A) Article 21
B) Article 32
C) Article 226
D) Article 19
Answer: B) Article 32

Q19. A Money Bill can be introduced only in the:
A) Rajya Sabha
B) Lok Sabha
C) Both Houses concurrently
D) State Legislative Council
Answer: B) Lok Sabha

Q20. Which Constitutional Amendment lowered the voting age for elections from 21 years to 18 years?
A) 42nd Amendment
B) 44th Amendment
C) 61st Amendment
D) 73rd Amendment
Answer: C) 61st Amendment
