International Current Affairs



Q1. Which country officially became the 31st member of the NATO alliance in April 2023?
A) Sweden
B) Finland
C) Ukraine
D) Georgia
Answer: B) Finland

Q2. Following Finland, which country formally joined NATO as its 32nd member in early 2024?
A) Sweden
B) Austria
C) Switzerland
D) Ireland
Answer: A) Sweden

Q3. Who was elected as the President of Argentina in the highly publicized 2023 presidential elections?
A) Alberto Fernández
B) Sergio Massa
C) Javier Milei
D) Mauricio Macri
Answer: C) Javier Milei

Q4. The COP28 (UN Climate Change Conference) in 2023 was held in which city?
A) Paris, France
B) Glasgow, UK
C) Sharm El Sheikh, Egypt
D) Dubai, UAE
Answer: D) Dubai, UAE

Q5. Which country assumed the presidency of the BRICS group for the year 2024?
A) Brazil
B) Russia
C) India
D) China
Answer: B) Russia

Q6. In May 2023, the World Health Organization (WHO) officially declared the end of the global health emergency for which disease?
A) Monkeypox (Mpox)
B) Ebola
C) COVID-19
D) Zika
Answer: C) COVID-19

Q7. The historic 'Bletchley Declaration', signed by several countries including the UK, US, and China in 2023, is associated with regulating which technology?
A) Quantum Computing
B) Artificial Intelligence (AI)
C) Cryptocurrency
D) Autonomous Weapons
Answer: B) Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Q8. Which country became the fifth nation to successfully soft-land a spacecraft (SLIM) on the moon in early 2024?

B) Israel

D) South Korea
Answer: A) Japan
