Indian Geography



Q1. Which is the longest river flowing entirely within India?
A) Brahmaputra
B) Ganga
C) Godavari
D) Yamuna
Answer: B) Ganga

Q2. Which Indian state has the longest coastline?
A) Maharashtra
B) Gujarat
C) Andhra Pradesh
D) Tamil Nadu
Answer: B) Gujarat

Q3. The highest mountain peak situated in undisputed Indian territory is:
A) Nanda Devi
B) Kangchenjunga
C) Anamudi
D) K2
Answer: B) Kangchenjunga

Q4. Majuli, recognized as the largest river island in the world, is located in which river?
A) Ganga
B) Brahmaputra
C) Godavari
D) Indus
Answer: B) Brahmaputra

Q5. Which of the following imaginary lines almost divides India into two equal northern and southern halves?
A) Equator
B) Tropic of Capricorn
C) Tropic of Cancer
D) Arctic Circle
Answer: C) Tropic of Cancer

Q6. Which state is popularly known as the 'Spice Garden of India'?
A) Karnataka
B) Kerala
C) Tamil Nadu
D) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: B) Kerala

Q7. The oldest mountain range in India is the:
A) Himalayas
B) Western Ghats
C) Aravalli Range
D) Vindhya Range
Answer: C) Aravalli Range

Q8. Which river is historically referred to as the 'Sorrow of Bihar' due to its frequent flooding?
A) Kosi
B) Son
C) Gandak
D) Damodar
Answer: A) Kosi

Q9. In which state is the Gir National Park, the only natural habitat of the Asiatic Lion, situated?
A) Madhya Pradesh
B) Rajasthan
C) Gujarat
D) Maharashtra
Answer: C) Gujarat

Q10. Which of the following is the largest freshwater lake in India?
A) Chilika Lake
B) Wular Lake
C) Loktak Lake
D) Vembanad Lake
Answer: B) Wular Lake

Q11. The Deccan Plateau is primarily formed of which type of rocks?
A) Sedimentary rocks
B) Basaltic (Igneous) rocks
C) Metamorphic rocks
D) Sandstone
Answer: B) Basaltic (Igneous) rocks

Q12. Which of the following mountain passes connects Srinagar to Leh?
A) Rohtang Pass
B) Nathu La
C) Zoji La
D) Shipki La
Answer: C) Zoji La

Q13. India shares its longest international land border with which country?
A) China
B) Pakistan
C) Nepal
D) Bangladesh
Answer: D) Bangladesh

Q14. The western coastal strip, south of Goa, is referred to as the:
A) Coromandel Coast
B) Konkan Coast
C) Malabar Coast
D) Northern Circars
Answer: C) Malabar Coast

Q15. Which of the following places in India receives the highest average annual rainfall?
A) Western Ghats
B) Mawsynram
C) Cherrapunji
D) Andaman and Nicobar Islands
Answer: B) Mawsynram

Q16. The state with the largest area under forest cover in India is:
A) Arunachal Pradesh
B) Chhattisgarh
C) Madhya Pradesh
D) Maharashtra
Answer: C) Madhya Pradesh

Q17. Which Indian state is the largest producer of wheat?
A) Punjab
B) Haryana
C) Uttar Pradesh
D) Madhya Pradesh
Answer: C) Uttar Pradesh

Q18. The Chilika Lake, the largest brackish water lagoon in India, is located in which state?
A) West Bengal
B) Odisha
C) Andhra Pradesh
D) Kerala
Answer: B) Odisha

Q19. What is the name of the southernmost point of India's territory (including islands)?
A) Kanyakumari
B) Indira Point
C) Point Calimere
D) Cape Comorin
Answer: B) Indira Point

Q20. Which of the following major rivers flows westward through a rift valley?
A) Narmada
B) Godavari
C) Krishna
D) Mahanadi
Answer: A) Narmada
