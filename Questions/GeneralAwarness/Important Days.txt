Important Days



Q1. International Women's Day is celebrated globally every year on:
A) March 8
B) April 8
C) May 8
D) June 8
Answer: A) March 8

Q2. National Youth Day in India is celebrated on January 12 to commemorate the birth anniversary of which great philosopher and monk?
A) Bhagat Singh
B) Swami Vivekananda
C) Subhas Chandra Bose
D) A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
Answer: B) Swami Vivekananda

Q3. World Environment Day, a flagship campaign for raising environmental awareness, is observed every year on:
A) 5th June
B) 5th July
C) 15th August
D) 22nd April
Answer: A) 5th June

Q4. In India, 'National Science Day' is celebrated on February 28 to mark the discovery of the:
A) Raman Effect by Sir C.V. Raman
B) Theory of Relativity by Albert Einstein
C) First Indian satellite, Aryabhata
D) DNA double helix structure
Answer: A) Raman Effect by Sir C.V. Raman

Q5. The International Day of Yoga is celebrated worldwide on which date, the longest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere?
A) June 21
B) July 21
C) March 21
D) September 21
Answer: A) June 21

Q6. World Health Day is observed by the World Health Organization (WHO) on which date every year?
A) April 7
B) May 1
C) October 24
D) December 1
Answer: A) April 7

Q7. In India, 'Teachers' Day' is celebrated on September 5 to mark the birth anniversary of which former President?
A) Jawaharlal Nehru
B) Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan
C) Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
D) Dr. Rajendra Prasad
Answer: B) Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan

Q8. Human Rights Day is observed globally to honor the adoption of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights on:
A) October 24
B) November 14
C) December 10
D) January 30
Answer: C) December 10

Q9. When is 'World Earth Day' celebrated to demonstrate support for environmental protection?
A) March 22
B) April 22
C) May 22
D) June 22
Answer: B) April 22

Q10. 'Pravasi Bharatiya Divas' (Non-Resident Indian Day) is celebrated on January 9 to mark the historic return of which leader to India from South Africa?
A) Subhas Chandra Bose
B) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
C) Mahatma Gandhi
D) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
Answer: C) Mahatma Gandhi

Q11. 'Good Governance Day' is observed in India on December 25 to mark the birth anniversary of which former Prime Minister?
A) Atal Bihari Vajpayee
B) Indira Gandhi
C) Lal Bahadur Shastri
D) Rajiv Gandhi
Answer: A) Atal Bihari Vajpayee

Q12. World Water Day, held annually to highlight the importance of freshwater, is observed globally on:
A) March 22
B) April 22
C) May 22
D) June 22
Answer: A) March 22

Q13. National Voters' Day is celebrated in India on which date to mark the foundation day of the Election Commission of India?
A) January 15
B) January 25
C) February 28
D) March 8
Answer: B) January 25

Q14. The United Nations Day is celebrated every year to mark the anniversary of the entry into force of the UN Charter in 1945 on:
A) September 11
B) October 24
C) November 14
D) December 10
Answer: B) October 24

Q15. In India, 'Children's Day' is celebrated on November 14 to commemorate the birth anniversary of:
A) Mahatma Gandhi
B) Jawaharlal Nehru
C) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
D) Mother Teresa
Answer: B) Jawaharlal Nehru

Q16. 'Parakram Diwas' (Day of Valor) is celebrated annually on January 23 to mark the birth anniversary of which prominent freedom fighter?
A) Bhagat Singh
B) Subhas Chandra Bose
C) Chandrashekhar Azad
D) Mangal Pandey
Answer: B) Subhas Chandra Bose

Q17. World AIDS Day, designated to unite people in the fight against HIV, is observed globally on:
A) November 1
B) December 1
C) January 1
D) February 1
Answer: B) December 1

Q18. National Girl Child Day in India is observed annually to raise awareness about the rights of a girl child on:
A) January 24
B) March 8
C) October 11
D) November 14
Answer: A) January 24

Q19. World Population Day is observed annually to raise awareness of global population issues on:
A) July 11
B) August 12
C) September 5
D) October 2
Answer: A) July 11

Q20. Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) in India is celebrated to commemorate the adoption of the Constitution of India on:
A) January 26
B) August 15
C) November 26
D) December 25
Answer: C) November 26
