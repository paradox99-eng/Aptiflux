Organizations & Headquarters



Q1. Where is the headquarters of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) located?
A) New Delhi
B) Mumbai
C) Kolkata
D) Chennai
Answer: B) Mumbai

Q2. The headquarters of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) is located in:
A) Geneva, Switzerland
B) New York, USA
C) Washington D.C., USA
D) Paris, France
Answer: C) Washington D.C., USA

Q3. Where is the headquarters of the World Bank located?
A) Washington D.C., USA
B) Geneva, Switzerland
C) London, UK
D) New York, USA
Answer: A) Washington D.C., USA

Q4. The headquarters of the World Health Organization (WHO) is situated in:
A) Rome, Italy
B) Paris, France
C) Geneva, Switzerland
D) Vienna, Austria
Answer: C) Geneva, Switzerland

Q5. Where is the headquarters of the United Nations (UN) formally located?
A) Geneva, Switzerland
B) The Hague, Netherlands
C) Washington D.C., USA
D) New York, USA
Answer: D) New York, USA

Q6. The headquarters of the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) is located in:
A) Thiruvananthapuram
B) Bengaluru
C) Hyderabad
D) Sriharikota
Answer: B) Bengaluru

Q7. Where is the headquarters of the World Trade Organization (WTO) located?
A) Geneva, Switzerland
B) Paris, France
C) New York, USA
D) London, UK
Answer: A) Geneva, Switzerland

Q8. The headquarters of the International Court of Justice (ICJ) is situated at:
A) Geneva, Switzerland
B) The Hague, Netherlands
C) Vienna, Austria
D) Paris, France
Answer: B) The Hague, Netherlands

Q9. Where is the headquarters of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO)?
A) Rome, Italy
B) Paris, France
C) Geneva, Switzerland
D) New York, USA
Answer: B) Paris, France

Q10. The headquarters of the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) is located in:
A) Bengaluru
B) Hyderabad
C) Pune
D) New Delhi
Answer: D) New Delhi

Q11. Where is the headquarters of the South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation (SAARC)?
A) New Delhi, India
B) Dhaka, Bangladesh
C) Kathmandu, Nepal
D) Colombo, Sri Lanka
Answer: C) Kathmandu, Nepal

Q12. The headquarters of the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) of the UN is located in:
A) Rome, Italy
B) Paris, France
C) Geneva, Switzerland
D) Vienna, Austria
Answer: A) Rome, Italy

Q13. Where is the headquarters of the Asian Development Bank (ADB) located?
A) Tokyo, Japan
B) Manila, Philippines
C) Beijing, China
D) Singapore
Answer: B) Manila, Philippines

Q14. The headquarters (secretariat) of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) is situated in:
A) Bangkok, Thailand
B) Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
C) Jakarta, Indonesia
D) Singapore
Answer: C) Jakarta, Indonesia

Q15. Where is the headquarters of the International Labour Organization (ILO)?
A) Geneva, Switzerland
B) Paris, France
C) New York, USA
D) Washington D.C., USA
Answer: A) Geneva, Switzerland

Q16. The headquarters of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) is located in:
A) Washington D.C., USA
B) London, UK
C) Brussels, Belgium
D) Paris, France
Answer: C) Brussels, Belgium

Q17. Where is the headquarters of the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI)?
A) New Delhi
B) Kolkata
C) Mumbai
D) Chennai
Answer: C) Mumbai

Q18. The headquarters of the International Criminal Police Organization (INTERPOL) is located in:
A) Lyon, France
B) London, UK
C) Geneva, Switzerland
D) New York, USA
Answer: A) Lyon, France

Q19. Where is the headquarters of the World Economic Forum (WEF) located?

Answer: A) Cologny (Geneva), Switzerland

Q20. The headquarters of the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) is located in:
