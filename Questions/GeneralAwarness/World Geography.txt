World Geography



Q1. Which is the largest continent in the world by land area?
A) Africa
B) North America
C) Asia
D) Europe
Answer: C) Asia

Q2. What is the capital city of Australia?
A) Sydney
B) Melbourne
C) Canberra
D) Perth
Answer: C) Canberra

Q3. Which is considered the longest river in the world?
A) Amazon River
B) Nile River
C) Yangtze River
D) Mississippi River
Answer: B) Nile River

Q4. The Sahara Desert, the largest hot desert in the world, is located in which continent?
A) South America
B) Africa
C) Asia
D) Australia
Answer: B) Africa

Q5. Which is the smallest ocean in the world?
A) Indian Ocean
B) Atlantic Ocean
C) Arctic Ocean
D) Southern Ocean
Answer: C) Arctic Ocean

Q6. Mount Everest, the highest mountain peak in the world, is located in which mountain range?
A) The Alps
B) The Andes
C) The Rockies
D) The Himalayas
Answer: D) The Himalayas

Q7. What is the capital of Canada?
A) Toronto
B) Vancouver
C) Ottawa
D) Montreal
Answer: C) Ottawa

Q8. Which country is popularly known as the 'Land of the Rising Sun'?
A) China
B) Japan
C) Thailand
D) South Korea
Answer: B) Japan

Q9. The Great Barrier Reef, the world's largest coral reef system, is situated off the coast of which country?
A) Brazil
B) South Africa
C) Australia
D) Indonesia
Answer: C) Australia

Q10. Which of the following is the largest country in the world by land area?
A) Canada
B) China
C) United States
D) Russia
Answer: D) Russia

Q11. The Andes mountain range, the longest continental mountain range in the world, is located in which continent?
A) North America
B) South America
C) Europe
D) Africa
Answer: B) South America

Q12. Which two countries share the longest international land border in the world?
A) Russia and China
B) United States and Canada
C) India and Bangladesh
D) Argentina and Chile
Answer: B) United States and Canada

Q13. What is the capital of Brazil?
A) Rio de Janeiro
B) São Paulo
C) Brasília
D) Buenos Aires
Answer: C) Brasília

Q14. The Mariana Trench, the deepest part of the world's oceans, is located in the:
A) Atlantic Ocean
B) Indian Ocean
C) Pacific Ocean
D) Arctic Ocean
Answer: C) Pacific Ocean

Q15. Which continent has historically been referred to as the 'Dark Continent'?
A) Asia
B) Africa
C) Antarctica
D) South America
Answer: B) Africa

Q16. The Amazon Rainforest is predominantly located in which country?
A) Peru
B) Colombia
C) Brazil
D) Venezuela
Answer: C) Brazil

Q17. Which famous river flows through Paris, the capital of France?
A) Thames
B) Seine
C) Rhine
D) Danube
Answer: B) Seine

Q18. The Strait of Gibraltar connects the Atlantic Ocean to the:
A) Red Sea
B) Black Sea
C) Mediterranean Sea
D) Baltic Sea
Answer: C) Mediterranean Sea

Q19. Which of the following is the deepest and oldest freshwater lake in the world?
A) Lake Superior
B) Lake Victoria
C) Lake Baikal
D) Caspian Sea
Answer: C) Lake Baikal

Q20. What is the capital city of Argentina?
A) Lima
B) Bogota
C) Santiago
D) Buenos Aires
Answer: D) Buenos Aires
