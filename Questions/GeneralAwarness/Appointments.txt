Appointments



Q1. Who was appointed as the President of the World Bank in 2023, becoming the first Indian-American to hold the position?
A) Raghuram Rajan
B) Ajay Banga
C) Sundar Pichai
D) Satya Nadella
Answer: B) Ajay Banga

Q2. Under the Indian Constitution, who appoints the Chief Justice of India?
A) Prime Minister
B) President of India
C) Parliament
D) Union Law Minister
Answer: B) President of India

Q3. Who is the current Secretary-General of the United Nations (serving his second term which began in 2022)?
A) Ban Ki-moon
B) Kofi Annan
C) António Guterres
D) Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus
Answer: C) António Guterres

Q4. In India, who appoints the Governor of a State?
A) Chief Minister of the State
B) Prime Minister
C) Chief Justice of India
D) President of India
Answer: D) President of India

Q5. Who was appointed as the first female and first African Director-General of the World Trade Organization (WTO)?
A) Christine Lagarde
B) Kristalina Georgieva
C) Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala
D) Gita Gopinath
Answer: C) Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala

Q6. Who appoints the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) of India?
A) Finance Minister
B) Prime Minister
C) President of India
D) Parliament
Answer: C) President of India

Q7. Who serves as the ex-officio Chairman of the National Institution for Transforming India (NITI Aayog)?
A) President of India
B) Finance Minister
C) Prime Minister
D) RBI Governor
Answer: C) Prime Minister

Q8. Who was appointed as the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of NITI Aayog in February 2023?
A) Amitabh Kant
B) Parameswaran Iyer
C) B. V. R. Subrahmanyam
D) Suman Bery
Answer: C) B. V. R. Subrahmanyam

Q9. The Chairman and other members of the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) are appointed by:
A) Prime Minister
B) President of India
C) Home Minister
D) Chief Justice of India
Answer: B) President of India

Q10. Who was elected as the President of the 78th session of the UN General Assembly (UNGA) in 2023?
A) Csaba Kőrösi
B) Dennis Francis
C) Abdulla Shahid
D) Volkan Bozkir
Answer: B) Dennis Francis

Q11. Who appoints the Attorney General for India, the highest law officer in the country?
A) Chief Justice of India
B) Ministry of Law and Justice
C) President of India
D) Parliament
Answer: C) President of India

Q12. Which Indian-origin political leader was appointed as the Prime Minister of the United Kingdom in October 2022?
A) Leo Varadkar
B) Rishi Sunak
C) Priti Patel
D) Suella Braverman
Answer: B) Rishi Sunak

Q13. Who became the first woman to be appointed as the Chairperson and CEO of the Railway Board in India in 2023?
A) Soma Mondal
B) Alka Mittal
C) Jaya Varma Sinha
D) Madhabi Puri Buch
Answer: C) Jaya Varma Sinha

Q14. Who was appointed as the first woman Chairperson of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)?
A) Arundhati Bhattacharya
B) Madhabi Puri Buch
C) Chanda Kochhar
D) Shikha Sharma
Answer: B) Madhabi Puri Buch

Q15. The Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) of the Indian Armed Forces is appointed by the:
A) Minister of Defence
B) President of India
C) Prime Minister
D) National Security Advisor
Answer: B) President of India

Q16. Who appoints the State Election Commissioner for conducting panchayat and municipal elections in India?
A) President of India
B) Chief Election Commissioner of India
C) Governor of the respective State
D) Chief Minister of the respective State
Answer: C) Governor of the respective State

Q17. Who was appointed as the Director-General of the World Health Organization (WHO) and re-elected for a second term in 2022?
A) Margaret Chan
B) Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus
C) Soumya Swaminathan
D) Anders Tegnell
Answer: B) Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus

Q18. The Chairman of the Finance Commission in India is appointed by the:
A) Finance Minister
B) Prime Minister
C) RBI Governor
D) President of India
Answer: D) President of India

Q19. Under Article 75 of the Indian Constitution, the Prime Minister is appointed by the:
A) Chief Justice of India
B) Parliament
C) President of India
D) Lok Sabha Speaker
Answer: C) President of India

Q20. Who was appointed as the 15th President of India in 2022, becoming the first person from a tribal community to hold the office?
A) Droupadi Murmu
B) Pratibha Patil
C) Meira Kumar
D) Anusuiya Uikey
Answer: A) Droupadi Murmu
