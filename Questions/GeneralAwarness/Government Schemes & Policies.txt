Government Schemes & Policies



Q1. What is the primary objective of the 'Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana' (PMJDY)?
A) To provide free housing to the rural poor
B) To ensure financial inclusion of all households by providing bank accounts
C) To provide free healthcare to senior citizens
D) To ensure free education for all children
Answer: B) To ensure financial inclusion of all households by providing bank accounts

Q2. 'Make in India', a major national initiative to boost domestic manufacturing and attract foreign investment, was launched in which year?
A) 2014
B) 2015
C) 2016
D) 2018
Answer: A) 2014

Q3. Which government scheme aims to provide clean cooking fuel (LPG connections) to women from Below Poverty Line (BPL) households?
A) Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana
B) Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana
C) Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi
D) Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana
Answer: B) Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana

Q4. The 'Ayushman Bharat Yojana' (PM-JAY) is primarily associated with which sector?
A) Agriculture
B) Education
C) Health
D) Infrastructure
Answer: C) Health

Q5. Under the 'PM-KISAN' (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) scheme, eligible farmer families are provided an annual income support of:
A) Rs. 4,000
B) Rs. 6,000
C) Rs. 8,000
D) Rs. 10,000
Answer: B) Rs. 6,000

Q6. The 'Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao' scheme was launched to address the issue of:
A) Malnutrition among children
B) Child marriage
C) Declining Child Sex Ratio (CSR)
D) Women's unemployment
Answer: C) Declining Child Sex Ratio (CSR)

Q7. Which scheme was launched to achieve universal sanitation coverage and make India Open Defecation Free (ODF)?
A) Swachh Bharat Abhiyan
B) AMRUT
C) Smart Cities Mission
D) HRIDAY
Answer: A) Swachh Bharat Abhiyan

Q8. 'Atal Pension Yojana' (APY) is mainly focused on providing pension benefits to:
A) Government employees
B) Organized sector workers
C) Unorganized sector workers
D) Armed forces personnel
Answer: C) Unorganized sector workers

Q9. The 'Digital India' campaign aims to:
A) Distribute free laptops to all college students
B) Transform India into a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy
C) Build IT parks exclusively in rural areas
D) Subsidize internet recharges for all citizens
Answer: B) Transform India into a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy

Q10. 'Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana' (PMMY) provides loans up to what amount for non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises?
A) Rs. 5 Lakhs
B) Rs. 10 Lakhs
C) Rs. 15 Lakhs
D) Rs. 20 Lakhs
Answer: B) Rs. 10 Lakhs

Q11. The 'Skill India Mission' was launched with the original aim to train over 40 crore people in India in different skills by which year?
A) 2020
B) 2022
C) 2025
D) 2030
Answer: B) 2022

Q12. The 'Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act' (MGNREGA) guarantees how many days of wage employment in a financial year to a rural household?
A) 50 days
B) 100 days
C) 150 days
D) 200 days
Answer: B) 100 days

Q13. What is the primary purpose of the 'Stand Up India' scheme?
A) To promote entrepreneurship among women and SC/ST communities
B) To promote sports among youth
C) To encourage startups in the defense sector
D) To provide scholarships for higher education
Answer: A) To promote entrepreneurship among women and SC/ST communities

Q14. 'Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana' (PMAY) is a flagship mission associated with providing:
A) Affordable housing for all
B) Free electricity connections
C) Subsidized crop insurance
D) Pension for the elderly
Answer: A) Affordable housing for all

Q15. The 'AMRUT' scheme stands for:
A) Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation
B) Asian Mission for Rural and Urban Transportation
C) Awas Mission for Rural and Urban Territories
D) Agriculture Mission for Rural Upliftment and Transformation
Answer: A) Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation

Q16. 'Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana' (PMFBY) is related to which of the following?
A) Life insurance
B) Health insurance
C) Crop insurance
D) Vehicle insurance
Answer: C) Crop insurance

Q17. Which initiative aims to provide a functional household tap water connection to every rural household?
A) Jal Jeevan Mission
B) Namami Gange
C) Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana
D) Swajal Yojana
Answer: A) Jal Jeevan Mission

Q18. The 'Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana' is a small deposit scheme exclusively intended for:
A) Senior citizens
B) A girl child
C) A boy child
D) Widows
Answer: B) A girl child

Q19. The 'UDAN' (Ude Desh ka Aam Naagrik) scheme is primarily related to:
A) Connecting unconnected regional airports and making flying affordable
B) Providing agricultural drones to farmers
C) Promoting aerospace engineering among girls
D) Upgrading major railway stations
Answer: A) Connecting unconnected regional airports and making flying affordable

Q20. The 'Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana' (PMGSY) aims to provide:
A) Free electricity to all remote villages
B) All-weather road connectivity to unconnected villages
C) Free broadband internet in villages
D) Piped drinking water to villages
Answer: B) All-weather road connectivity to unconnected villages
