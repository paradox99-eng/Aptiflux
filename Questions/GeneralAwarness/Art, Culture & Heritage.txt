Art, Culture & Heritage



Q1. Which of the following classical dance forms originated in the state of Kerala?
A) Kathak
B) Bharatanatyam
C) Kathakali
D) Odissi
Answer: C) Kathakali

Q2. 'Garba' is a highly popular and energetic folk dance of which Indian state?
A) Punjab
B) Gujarat
C) Rajasthan
D) Maharashtra
Answer: B) Gujarat

Q3. The famous 'Hornbill Festival', often called the 'Festival of Festivals', is celebrated in which state of India?
A) Assam
B) Meghalaya
C) Nagaland
D) Manipur
Answer: C) Nagaland

Q4. Which Indian classical dance form is historically and culturally associated with the state of Tamil Nadu?
A) Bharatanatyam
B) Kuchipudi
C) Manipuri
D) Mohiniyattam
Answer: A) Bharatanatyam

Q5. The Sun Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage site known for its massive chariot-like architecture, is located at:
A) Khajuraho
B) Konark
C) Hampi
D) Mahabalipuram
Answer: B) Konark

Q6. 'Madhubani' is a famous style of folk painting that is predominantly popular in which state?
A) Bihar
B) Rajasthan
C) Odisha
D) West Bengal
Answer: A) Bihar

Q7. Bihu is a major traditional set of festivals celebrated primarily in:
A) Tripura
B) Assam
C) Sikkim
D) Arunachal Pradesh
Answer: B) Assam

Q8. Which of the following is a classical wind instrument of India?
A) Sitar
B) Tabla
C) Shehnai
D) Sarod
Answer: C) Shehnai

Q9. The Ajanta and Ellora caves, world-famous for their ancient rock-cut architecture and exquisite frescoes, are situated in:
A) Madhya Pradesh
B) Gujarat
C) Maharashtra
D) Karnataka
Answer: C) Maharashtra

Q10. 'Bhangra' is a lively, rhythmic, and energetic folk dance belonging to which state?
A) Haryana
B) Punjab
C) Himachal Pradesh
D) Uttarakhand
Answer: B) Punjab

Q11. The Indian classical music system is broadly divided into two major distinct traditions: Hindustani music (North Indian) and:
A) Sufi music
B) Carnatic music
C) Ghazal
D) Baul music
Answer: B) Carnatic music

Q12. 'Kuchipudi' is a classical dance form that originated in the villages of:
A) Andhra Pradesh
B) Kerala
C) Tamil Nadu
D) Odisha
Answer: A) Andhra Pradesh

Q13. The famous 'Pushkar Fair', an annual multi-day livestock fair and vibrant cultural festival, is held in:
A) Gujarat
B) Rajasthan
C) Haryana
D) Punjab
Answer: B) Rajasthan

Q14. Who among the following is a legendary, world-renowned Indian classical sitar player?
A) Ustad Bismillah Khan
B) Pandit Ravi Shankar
C) Ustad Zakir Hussain
D) Pandit Hariprasad Chaurasia
Answer: B) Pandit Ravi Shankar

Q15. The Khajuraho Group of Monuments, famous worldwide for their stunning Nagara-style architecture and intricate sculptures, are located in:
A) Uttar Pradesh
B) Madhya Pradesh
C) Rajasthan
D) Bihar
Answer: B) Madhya Pradesh

Q16. 'Sattriya' is one of the major Indian classical dance forms recognized by the Sangeet Natak Akademi. It belongs to which state?
A) Assam
B) Manipur
C) West Bengal
D) Jharkhand
Answer: A) Assam

Q17. 'Warli' painting is a beautiful indigenous tribal art form that predominantly originates from which state?
A) Madhya Pradesh
B) Chhattisgarh
C) Maharashtra
D) Jharkhand
Answer: C) Maharashtra

Q18. The traditional martial art form 'Kalaripayattu', considered one of the oldest surviving martial arts, originated in which state?
A) Tamil Nadu
B) Kerala
C) Karnataka
D) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: B) Kerala

Q19. 'Onam' is a major cultural harvest festival celebrated with great enthusiasm in:
A) Kerala
B) Tamil Nadu
C) Karnataka
D) Telangana
Answer: A) Kerala

Q20. The Sanchi Stupa, one of the oldest surviving stone structures in India originally commissioned by Emperor Ashoka, is located in:
A) Bihar
B) Uttar Pradesh
C) Madhya Pradesh
D) Gujarat
Answer: C) Madhya Pradesh
