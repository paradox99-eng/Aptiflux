General Science



Q1. What is the Standard International (SI) unit of electric current?
A) Volt
B) Ampere
C) Ohm
D) Watt
Answer: B) Ampere

Q2. The speed of light in a vacuum is approximately:
A) 300,000 m/s
B) 3,000,000 m/s
C) 30,000,000 m/s
D) 300,000,000 m/s
Answer: D) 300,000,000 m/s

Q3. What is the chemical formula for common table salt?
A) KCl
B) NaCl
C) NaHCO3
D) CaCO3
Answer: B) NaCl

Q4. Which cellular organelle is popularly known as the "powerhouse of the cell"?
A) Nucleus
B) Ribosome
C) Mitochondria
D) Endoplasmic Reticulum
Answer: C) Mitochondria

Q5. Which gas is the most abundant in the Earth's atmosphere?
A) Oxygen
B) Carbon Dioxide
C) Nitrogen
D) Hydrogen
Answer: C) Nitrogen

Q6. The pH value of pure (neutral) water at room temperature is:
A) 5
B) 6
C) 7
D) 8
Answer: C) 7

Q7. What is the largest organ in the human body?
A) Heart
B) Liver
C) Brain
D) Skin
Answer: D) Skin

Q8. Which of Newton's laws of motion states that "for every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction"?
A) First Law
B) Second Law
C) Third Law
D) Law of Universal Gravitation
Answer: C) Third Law

Q9. What is the lightest element in the periodic table?
A) Helium
B) Lithium
C) Oxygen
D) Hydrogen
Answer: D) Hydrogen

Q10. The pigment that gives human skin, hair, and eyes their color is called:
A) Melanin
B) Carotene
C) Hemoglobin
D) Chlorophyll
Answer: A) Melanin

Q11. Which vitamin is naturally synthesized in the human body when the skin is exposed to sunlight?
A) Vitamin A
B) Vitamin B12
C) Vitamin C
D) Vitamin D
Answer: D) Vitamin D

Q12. Sound waves are mechanical waves and therefore cannot travel through:
A) Solids
B) Liquids
C) Gases
D) A vacuum
Answer: D) A vacuum

Q13. The process of a solid changing directly into a gas without passing through the liquid state is called:
A) Evaporation
B) Condensation
C) Sublimation
D) Melting
Answer: C) Sublimation

Q14. In the ABO blood group system, which blood type is universally known as the "Universal Donor"?
A) Type A
B) Type B
C) Type AB
D) Type O
Answer: D) Type O

Q15. Which instrument is used to measure atmospheric pressure?
A) Thermometer
B) Barometer
C) Hygrometer
D) Anemometer
Answer: B) Barometer

Q16. Which noble gas is commonly used in standard incandescent light bulbs to prevent the tungsten filament from oxidizing?
A) Helium
B) Neon
C) Argon
D) Radon
Answer: C) Argon

Q17. How many chromosomes are there in a normal human somatic (body) cell?
A) 23
B) 44
C) 46
D) 48
Answer: C) 46

Q18. What is the primary chemical component of natural gas?
A) Ethane
B) Propane
C) Butane
D) Methane
Answer: D) Methane

Q19. The tendency of a liquid drop to contract and occupy the minimum possible surface area is due to:
A) Viscosity
B) Surface Tension
C) Density
D) Buoyancy
Answer: B) Surface Tension

Q20. Which disease is caused by a prolonged deficiency of Vitamin C in the diet?
A) Scurvy
B) Rickets
C) Beriberi
D) Night blindness
Answer: A) Scurvy
