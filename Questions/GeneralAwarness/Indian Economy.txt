Indian Economy



Q1. Which organization is responsible for estimating the National Income and GDP in India?
A) Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
B) Ministry of Finance
C) National Statistical Office (NSO)
D) NITI Aayog
Answer: C) National Statistical Office (NSO)

Q2. The official financial year in India runs from:
A) 1st January to 31st December
B) 1st April to 31st March
C) 1st July to 30th June
D) 1st October to 30th September
Answer: B) 1st April to 31st March

Q3. The Goods and Services Tax (GST) was implemented in India in which year?
A) 2015
B) 2016
C) 2017
D) 2018
Answer: C) 2017

Q4. What does the term 'CRR' stand for in banking terminology?
A) Cash Reserve Ratio
B) Current Return Rate
C) Core Reserve Rate
D) Credit Rationing Rate
Answer: A) Cash Reserve Ratio

Q5. Which prominent body was replaced by the NITI Aayog in 2015?
A) Finance Commission
B) Planning Commission
C) National Development Council
D) Reserve Bank of India
Answer: B) Planning Commission

Q6. In India, the one-rupee note and coins are issued by the:
A) Reserve Bank of India
B) State Bank of India
C) Ministry of Finance
D) NITI Aayog
Answer: C) Ministry of Finance

Q7. Which of the following taxes is an example of a Direct Tax?
A) Goods and Services Tax (GST)
B) Custom Duty
C) Excise Duty
D) Income Tax
Answer: D) Income Tax

Q8. Currently, inflation targeting by the RBI in India is primarily measured on the basis of:
A) Wholesale Price Index (WPI)
B) Consumer Price Index (CPI)
C) Gross Domestic Product (GDP) Deflator
D) Sensex
Answer: B) Consumer Price Index (CPI)

Q9. The 'Repo Rate' is the rate at which:
A) Commercial banks lend money to the RBI
B) The RBI lends short-term money to commercial banks
C) The general public borrows from commercial banks
D) The Government borrows from the World Bank
Answer: B) The RBI lends short-term money to commercial banks

Q10. Which sector is the largest contributor to India's Gross Domestic Product (GDP)?
A) Primary Sector (Agriculture)
B) Secondary Sector (Industry)
C) Tertiary Sector (Services)
D) Quaternary Sector
Answer: C) Tertiary Sector (Services)

Q11. 'Economic planning' is a subject that falls under which list in the Indian Constitution?
A) Union List
B) State List
C) Concurrent List
D) Unspecified List
Answer: C) Concurrent List

Q12. A 'Mixed Economy' refers to an economic system where:
A) Both agriculture and industry are equally promoted
B) There is a co-existence of public and private sectors
C) There is a mix of domestic and foreign investments
D) Heavy and light industries operate together
Answer: B) There is a co-existence of public and private sectors

Q13. The concept of Five-Year Plans in the Indian economy was originally borrowed from:
A) USA
B) UK
C) USSR (Soviet Union)
D) France
Answer: C) USSR (Soviet Union)

Q14. SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) is a:
A) Statutory body
B) Constitutional body
C) Non-statutory body
D) Advisory body
Answer: A) Statutory body

Q15. The terms 'Bulls and Bears' are associated with which of the following?
A) Agricultural Trading
B) Banking Regulations
C) Stock Market
D) Foreign Trade
Answer: C) Stock Market

Q16. The first major phase of Bank Nationalisation in India, involving 14 major commercial banks, took place in the year:
A) 1955
B) 1969
C) 1980
D) 1991
Answer: B) 1969

Q17. What is the primary objective of the monetary policy in India?
A) To generate maximum employment
B) To maintain price stability while keeping in mind the objective of growth
C) To completely eradicate poverty
D) To increase government tax collection
Answer: B) To maintain price stability while keeping in mind the objective of growth

Q18. The 'Navratna' status is awarded by the Government of India to:
A) Outstanding private companies
B) Highly profitable Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs)
C) The top 9 agricultural export products
D) Leading foreign multinational companies
Answer: B) Highly profitable Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs)

Q19. What is meant by a 'Fiscal Deficit'?
A) Total revenue receipts minus total revenue expenditure
B) Total expenditure of the government minus total receipts excluding borrowings
C) The difference between total exports and imports
D) A deficit in the current account balance
Answer: B) Total expenditure of the government minus total receipts excluding borrowings

Q20. Who serves as the ex-officio Chairman of the NITI Aayog?
A) The President of India
B) The Finance Minister
C) The Prime Minister of India
D) The RBI Governor
Answer: C) The Prime Minister of India
