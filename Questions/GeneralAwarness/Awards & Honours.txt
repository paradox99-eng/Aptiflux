Awards & Honours

Directions: Read the following questions based on national and international awards carefully and select the correct option.

Q1. Which of the following is the highest civilian award of India?
A) Padma Vibhushan
B) Bharat Ratna
C) Padma Bhushan
D) Param Vir Chakra
Answer: B) Bharat Ratna

Q2. The Nobel Prize is NOT awarded in which of the following fields?
A) Physics
B) Peace
C) Mathematics
D) Literature
Answer: C) Mathematics

Q3. Which of the following awards is widely considered the equivalent of the Nobel Prize for Mathematics?
A) Fields Medal
B) Pulitzer Prize
C) Booker Prize
D) Turing Award
Answer: A) Fields Medal

Q4. The Grammy Award is a prestigious international award given in the field of:
A) Journalism
B) Music
C) Cinema
D) Literature
Answer: B) Music

Q5. Which is the highest gallantry award in India given for peacetime bravery?
A) Param Vir Chakra
B) Ashoka Chakra
C) Maha Vir Chakra
D) Kirti Chakra
Answer: B) Ashoka Chakra

Q6. The prestigious Pulitzer Prize is primarily associated with excellence in:
A) Science and Technology
B) Journalism and Literature
C) Sports
D) Environmental activism
Answer: B) Journalism and Literature

Q7. Who was the first Indian citizen to win a Nobel Prize?
A) C. V. Raman
B) Mother Teresa
C) Rabindranath Tagore
D) Amartya Sen
Answer: C) Rabindranath Tagore

Q8. The 'Oscar Awards', formally known as the Academy Awards, are presented for excellence in:
A) Theatre
B) Television
C) Cinema
D) Advertising
Answer: C) Cinema

Q9. The Ramon Magsaysay Award, often called the "Nobel Prize of Asia", is named in honor of the former President of which country?
A) Indonesia
B) Malaysia
C) Philippines
D) Thailand
Answer: C) Philippines

Q10. Which of the following awards is the highest literary honor given in India?
A) Sahitya Akademi Award
B) Jnanpith Award
C) Saraswati Samman
D) Vyas Samman
Answer: B) Jnanpith Award

Q11. In India, the 'Dronacharya Award' is given to:
A) Outstanding athletes
B) Promising sportspersons
C) Outstanding sports coaches
D) Sports administrators
Answer: C) Outstanding sports coaches

Q12. The prestigious 'Man Booker Prize' is awarded for the best original novel written in:
A) French
B) Spanish
C) English
D) German
Answer: C) English

Q13. Who among the following was one of the first recipients of the Bharat Ratna award in 1954?
A) Jawaharlal Nehru
B) C. Rajagopalachari
C) Dr. Rajendra Prasad
D) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
Answer: B) C. Rajagopalachari

Q14. The 'Turing Award', widely considered the "Nobel Prize of Computing", is given for outstanding contributions to:
A) Mathematics
B) Computer Science
C) Architecture
D) Robotics
Answer: B) Computer Science

Q15. The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is India's highest official award in the field of:
A) Music
B) Literature
C) Cinema
D) Sports
Answer: C) Cinema

Q16. The 'Kalinga Prize', created by UNESCO, is given for exceptional contribution to:
A) The popularization of science
B) Classical literature
C) Peacekeeping efforts
D) Human rights
Answer: A) The popularization of science

Q17. Who was the first woman in the world to win a Nobel Prize?
A) Mother Teresa
B) Rosalind Franklin
C) Marie Curie
D) Jane Addams
Answer: C) Marie Curie

Q18. The 'Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Award' in India is presented for outstanding contribution to:
A) Literature
B) Science and Technology
C) Social Work
D) Journalism
Answer: B) Science and Technology

Q19. Which of the following is the highest wartime gallantry award (military decoration) in India?
A) Ashoka Chakra
B) Param Vir Chakra
C) Maha Vir Chakra
D) Shaurya Chakra
Answer: B) Param Vir Chakra

Q20. The Arjuna Award, instituted by the Government of India in 1961, is given for outstanding performance in:
A) Science
B) Art
C) Sports
D) Architecture
Answer: C) Sports
