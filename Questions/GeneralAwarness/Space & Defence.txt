Space & Defence



Q1. What does the acronym 'ISRO' stand for?
A) Indian Scientific Research Organisation
B) Indian Space Research Organisation
C) International Space Research Organisation
D) Indian Satellite Research Organisation
Answer: B) Indian Space Research Organisation

Q2. Which premier organization is primarily responsible for military research and development in India?
A) ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation)
B) HAL (Hindustan Aeronautics Limited)
C) DRDO (Defence Research and Development Organisation)
D) BHEL (Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited)
Answer: C) DRDO (Defence Research and Development Organisation)

Q3. Where is the headquarters of ISRO located?
A) New Delhi
B) Hyderabad
C) Mumbai
D) Bengaluru
Answer: D) Bengaluru

Q4. What is the name of India's first completely indigenous satellite, launched in 1975?
A) Bhaskara
B) Rohini
C) Aryabhata
D) INSAT-1A
Answer: C) Aryabhata

Q5. Which Indian missile is a highly advanced supersonic cruise missile jointly developed with Russia?
A) Agni
B) Prithvi
C) BrahMos
D) Nag
Answer: C) BrahMos

Q6. The Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC), ISRO's primary spaceport and launch centre, is located in:
A) Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala
B) Sriharikota, Andhra Pradesh
C) Chandipur, Odisha
D) Balasore, Odisha
Answer: B) Sriharikota, Andhra Pradesh

Q7. What is the name of the indigenous Light Combat Aircraft (LCA) developed by India for the Indian Air Force?
A) Sukhoi
B) Tejas
C) Rafale
D) Mirage
Answer: B) Tejas

Q8. What was the primary destination and objective of ISRO's Mangalyaan mission?
A) To study the Sun's corona
B) To orbit and explore Mars
C) To soft-land on the Moon
D) To reach the orbit of Jupiter
Answer: B) To orbit and explore Mars

Q9. Which of the following is India's first indigenously designed and built nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine?
A) INS Vikramaditya
B) INS Kalvari
C) INS Chakra
D) INS Arihant
Answer: D) INS Arihant

Q10. 'Gaganyaan' is the flagship and highly ambitious mission of ISRO specifically designed to:
A) Send a robotic rover to Venus
B) Build an independent Indian space station
C) Send a human crew to Low Earth Orbit and bring them back safely
D) Study distant black holes and galaxies
Answer: C) Send a human crew to Low Earth Orbit and bring them back safely

Q11. The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) was established in which year?
A) 1947
B) 1958
C) 1969
D) 1998
Answer: B) 1958

Q12. What type of weapon system is India's 'Agni-V'?
A) Intercontinental Ballistic Missile (ICBM)
B) Nuclear-powered Submarine
C) Stealth Fighter Jet
D) Main Battle Tank
Answer: A) Intercontinental Ballistic Missile (ICBM)

Q13. In the Indian defense arsenal, what is 'Akash'?
A) A medium-range Surface-to-Air Missile (SAM) system
B) An Anti-Tank Guided Missile
C) An Air-to-Air Missile
D) A Naval stealth torpedo
Answer: A) A medium-range Surface-to-Air Missile (SAM) system

Q14. The Chandrayaan-1 lunar mission famously made the groundbreaking discovery of what on the Moon?
A) Massive gold deposits
B) Flowing underground lava
C) Evidence of water molecules
D) Fossilized alien life
Answer: C) Evidence of water molecules

Q15. India's first dedicated multi-wavelength space observatory satellite launched by ISRO is called:
A) Cartosat
B) Astrosat
C) RISAT
D) GSAT
Answer: B) Astrosat

Q16. Which organization is the premier intelligence agency of India dealing specifically with external threats and foreign intelligence?
A) IB (Intelligence Bureau)
B) CBI (Central Bureau of Investigation)
C) RAW (Research and Analysis Wing)
D) NIA (National Investigation Agency)
Answer: C) RAW (Research and Analysis Wing)

Q17. What is the name of the robotic rover successfully deployed by the Chandrayaan-3 mission on the lunar surface?
A) Vikram
B) Pragyan
C) Aditya
D) Vyommitra
Answer: B) Pragyan

Q18. The 'S-400 Triumf' is an advanced long-range air defence missile system that India procured from which country?
A) USA
B) France
C) Israel
D) Russia
Answer: D) Russia

Q19. What is 'Pinaka' in the context of the Indian Army's weapon systems?
A) An indigenous Main Battle Tank
B) A Multi-Barrel Rocket Launcher system
C) A stealth naval frigate
D) An unmanned combat aerial vehicle (UCAV)
Answer: B) A Multi-Barrel Rocket Launcher system

Q20. Which heavy-lift launch vehicle, formerly known as GSLV Mk III, was used by ISRO to launch the Chandrayaan-3 mission?
A) PSLV
B) ASLV
C) SSLV
D) LVM3 (Launch Vehicle Mark-3)
Answer: D) LVM3 (Launch Vehicle Mark-3)
