Indian History



Q1. Which of the following is the oldest of the four Vedas?
A) Samaveda
B) Yajurveda
C) Rigveda
D) Atharvaveda
Answer: C) Rigveda

Q2. The Harappan Civilization belongs to which of the following ages?
A) Iron Age
B) Bronze Age
C) Paleolithic Age
D) Neolithic Age
Answer: B) Bronze Age

Q3. Who was the founder of the Maurya Empire?
A) Ashoka
B) Bindusara
C) Chandragupta Maurya
D) Bimbisara
Answer: C) Chandragupta Maurya

Q4. Where did Gautama Buddha deliver his first sermon?
A) Bodh Gaya
B) Lumbini
C) Sarnath
D) Kushinagar
Answer: C) Sarnath

Q5. Who was the famous court physician of the Kushan king Kanishka?
A) Charaka
B) Sushruta
C) Dhanvantari
D) Patanjali
Answer: A) Charaka

Q6. What was the capital of the Pallava dynasty?
A) Madurai
B) Kanchipuram
C) Tanjore
D) Badami
Answer: B) Kanchipuram

Q7. Who is the author of the ancient Indian political treatise 'Arthashastra'?
A) Megasthenes
B) Kalidasa
C) Chanakya
D) Banabhatta
Answer: C) Chanakya

Q8. Who was the first and only female ruler of the Delhi Sultanate?
A) Chand Bibi
B) Razia Sultana
C) Nur Jahan
D) Mumtaz Mahal
Answer: B) Razia Sultana

Q9. The First Battle of Panipat (1526) was fought between Babur and:
A) Rana Sanga
B) Ibrahim Lodi
C) Hemu
D) Daulat Khan Lodi
Answer: B) Ibrahim Lodi

Q10. Which Mughal Emperor abolished the discriminatory Jizya tax on non-Muslims?
A) Babur
B) Humayun
C) Akbar
D) Aurangzeb
Answer: C) Akbar

Q11. The Vijayanagara Empire was founded by which of the following pairs?
A) Harihara and Bukka
B) Krishnadevaraya and Achyuta Deva Raya
C) Shivaji and Sambhaji
D) Rajaraja Chola and Rajendra Chola
Answer: A) Harihara and Bukka

Q12. Who built the famous Charminar in Hyderabad?
A) Quli Qutb Shah
B) Tipu Sultan
C) Asaf Jah I
D) Nizam Ali Khan
Answer: A) Quli Qutb Shah

Q13. The prominent Bhakti movement saint Kabir was a disciple of:
A) Ramanuja
B) Ramananda
C) Chaitanya Mahaprabhu
D) Vallabhacharya
Answer: B) Ramananda

Q14. In which year did the Battle of Plassey, which established British political power in India, take place?
A) 1757
B) 1764
C) 1857
D) 1905
Answer: A) 1757

Q15. Who was the first Indian Governor-General of independent India?
A) Lord Mountbatten
B) C. Rajagopalachari
C) Rajendra Prasad
D) Jawaharlal Nehru
Answer: B) C. Rajagopalachari

Q16. The Indian National Congress was founded in the year:
A) 1885
B) 1905
C) 1919
D) 1947
Answer: A) 1885

Q17. Who among the following is widely known as the 'Iron Man of India'?
A) Subhas Chandra Bose
B) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
C) Bal Gangadhar Tilak
D) Lala Lajpat Rai
Answer: B) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

Q18. The Non-Cooperation Movement was launched by Mahatma Gandhi in the year:
A) 1919
B) 1920
C) 1922
D) 1930
Answer: B) 1920

Q19. Who was the Viceroy of India during the Partition of Bengal in 1905?
A) Lord Dalhousie
B) Lord Curzon
C) Lord Ripon
D) Lord Minto
Answer: B) Lord Curzon

Q20. The famous 'Quit India Movement' was launched in which year?
A) 1930
B) 1940
C) 1942
D) 1945
Answer: C) 1942
