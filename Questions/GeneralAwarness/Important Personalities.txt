Important Personalities

Directions: Read the following questions carefully and select the most appropriate option.

Q1. Who is popularly known as the 'Missile Man of India'?
A) Homi J. Bhabha
B) Vikram Sarabhai
C) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam
D) C. V. Raman
Answer: C) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Q2. Which prominent leader of the Indian independence movement is referred to as 'Frontier Gandhi'?
A) Maulana Abul Kalam Azad
B) Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan
C) Sheikh Abdullah
D) Muhammad Ali Jinnah
Answer: B) Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan

Q3. Who was the first woman to serve as the Prime Minister of India?
A) Sarojini Naidu
B) Pratibha Patil
C) Indira Gandhi
D) Sucheta Kriplani
Answer: C) Indira Gandhi

Q4. The famous physicist Albert Einstein is best known worldwide for developing which theory?
A) Theory of Relativity
B) Quantum Mechanics
C) Laws of Motion
D) Theory of Evolution
Answer: A) Theory of Relativity

Q5. Which prominent Indian freedom fighter and poet is known as the 'Nightingale of India'?
A) Lata Mangeshkar
B) Sarojini Naidu
C) Asha Bhosle
D) Annie Besant
Answer: B) Sarojini Naidu

Q6. Nelson Mandela, a globally revered anti-apartheid revolutionary, served as the President of which country?
A) Kenya
B) South Africa
C) Nigeria
D) Ghana
Answer: B) South Africa

Q7. Who was the first person to step foot on the moon?
A) Yuri Gagarin
B) Buzz Aldrin
C) Neil Armstrong
D) Michael Collins
Answer: C) Neil Armstrong

Q8. Which famous personality is historically known as the 'Iron Man of India' for his role in the political integration of the country?
A) Subhas Chandra Bose
B) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
C) Bal Gangadhar Tilak
D) Bhagat Singh
Answer: B) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

Q9. Who among the following is universally known as the 'Father of the Indian Nation'?
A) Jawaharlal Nehru
B) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
C) Mahatma Gandhi
D) Dr. Rajendra Prasad
Answer: C) Mahatma Gandhi

Q10. Mother Teresa, famous for her lifelong humanitarian work in Calcutta, was originally born in which modern-day country?
A) Italy
B) North Macedonia
C) Albania
D) Greece
Answer: B) North Macedonia

Q11. Who holds the distinction of being the first Indian citizen to travel into space?
A) Kalpana Chawla
B) Sunita Williams
C) Rakesh Sharma
D) Ravish Malhotra
Answer: C) Rakesh Sharma

Q12. Which famous English playwright wrote the legendary tragedies 'Romeo and Juliet' and 'Hamlet'?
A) Charles Dickens
B) William Shakespeare
C) George Bernard Shaw
D) Oscar Wilde
Answer: B) William Shakespeare

Q13. Who is officially considered the principal architect of the Indian Constitution?
A) B. N. Rau
B) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
C) Jawaharlal Nehru
D) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
Answer: B) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

Q14. Swami Vivekananda famously represented India and Hinduism at the Parliament of the World's Religions in 1893 in which city?
A) London
B) New York
C) Chicago
D) Paris
Answer: C) Chicago

Q15. Who was the founder of the Maurya Empire in ancient India?
A) Ashoka
B) Chandragupta Maurya
C) Bindusara
D) Chanakya
Answer: B) Chandragupta Maurya

Q16. The famous Marxist revolutionary 'Che Guevara', who played a major role in the Cuban Revolution, was originally from which country?
A) Cuba
B) Bolivia
C) Argentina
D) Mexico
Answer: C) Argentina

Q17. Which Scottish-born inventor, scientist, and engineer is credited with inventing the first practical telephone?
A) Thomas Edison
B) Alexander Graham Bell
C) Nikola Tesla
D) Guglielmo Marconi
Answer: B) Alexander Graham Bell

Q18. Which iconic Indian leader famously gave the slogan "Give me blood and I will give you freedom"?
A) Mahatma Gandhi
B) Jawaharlal Nehru
C) Subhas Chandra Bose
D) Chandrashekhar Azad
Answer: C) Subhas Chandra Bose

Q19. Marie Curie, the pioneering scientist and two-time Nobel laureate, is famous for her discovery of:
A) Penicillin
B) Polonium and Radium
C) X-rays
D) The structure of DNA
Answer: B) Polonium and Radium

Q20. Who among the following scientists is widely known as the 'Father of the Indian Space Programme'?
A) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam
B) Homi J. Bhabha
C) Vikram Sarabhai
D) Satish Dhawan
Answer: C) Vikram Sarabhai
