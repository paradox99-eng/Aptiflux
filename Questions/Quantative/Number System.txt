Divisibility

Q1. Which of the following numbers is completely divisible by 99?
A) 913464
B) 114345
C) 135792
D) 3572404
Answer: B) 114345

Q2. If the number 653xy is perfectly divisible by 90, then what is the value of (x + y)?
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 6
Answer: C) 4

Q3. What least value must be assigned to '*' so that the number 8632*56 is divisible by 11?
A) 2
B) 5
C) 7
D) 9
Answer: C) 7

Q4. A number is always divisible by 11 if the difference between the sum of digits in its odd places and even places is:
A) 0 only
B) A multiple of 11 only
C) 0 or a multiple of 11
D) An even number
Answer: C) 0 or a multiple of 11

Q5. How many numbers between 200 and 600 are exactly divisible by 4, 5, and 6?
A) 5
B) 6
C) 7
D) 8
Answer: B) 6


Factors & Multiples

Q6. Find the total number of prime factors in the expression: (2^4) x (3^6) x (5^2) x 7.
A) 11
B) 12
C) 13
D) 14
Answer: C) 13

Q7. How many total factors does the number 120 have?
A) 12
B) 14
C) 16
D) 18
Answer: C) 16

Q8. The sum of all factors of the number 24 is:
A) 50
B) 60
C) 65
D) 72
Answer: B) 60

Q9. Which of the following numbers is a prime number?
A) 143
B) 289
C) 117
D) 131
Answer: D) 131

Q10. The product of any two consecutive even numbers is always perfectly divisible by:
A) 4
B) 6
C) 8
D) 12
Answer: C) 8


Unit Digits

Q11. Find the unit digit in the product: (2467)^153 x (341)^72
A) 1
B) 3
C) 7
D) 9
Answer: C) 7

Q12. What is the unit digit of 3^40?
A) 1
B) 3
C) 7
D) 9
Answer: A) 1

Q13. The unit digit in the sum of (124)^372 + (124)^373 is:
A) 0
B) 2
C) 4
D) 8
Answer: A) 0

Q14. Find the unit digit of the expression: 7^95 - 3^58
A) 0
B) 4
C) 6
D) 7
Answer: B) 4

Q15. What is the unit digit of 2^51?
A) 2
B) 4
C) 6
D) 8
Answer: D) 8


Remainders

Q16. A number when divided by 136 leaves a remainder of 36. If the same number is divided by 17, the remainder will be:
A) 2
B) 3
C) 5
D) 7
Answer: A) 2

Q17. Find the remainder when 2^31 is divided by 5.
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 4
Answer: C) 3

Q18. What is the remainder when 17^200 is divided by 18?
A) 1
B) 2
C) 16
D) 17
Answer: A) 1

Q19. A number when successively divided by 3, 5 and 8 leaves remainders 1, 4 and 7 respectively. Find the respective remainders if the order of divisors is reversed (divided by 8, then 5, then 3).
A) 4, 6, 2
B) 6, 4, 2
C) 2, 4, 6
D) 6, 2, 4
Answer: B) 6, 4, 2

Q20. When an integer 'n' is divided by 4, the remainder is 3. What is the remainder when '2n' is divided by 4?
A) 0
B) 1
C) 2
D) 3
Answer: C) 2
