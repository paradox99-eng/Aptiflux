Geometry

Q1. If an angle is 30 degrees less than its supplement, what is the measure of the angle?
A) 45 degrees
B) 60 degrees
C) 75 degrees
D) 105 degrees
Answer: C) 75 degrees

Q2. Two complementary angles are in the ratio 4:5. The larger angle is:
A) 40 degrees
B) 50 degrees
C) 80 degrees
D) 100 degrees
Answer: B) 50 degrees

Q3. The sum of the interior angles of a regular hexagon is:
A) 360 degrees
B) 540 degrees
C) 720 degrees
D) 900 degrees
Answer: C) 720 degrees

Q4. What is the measure of each exterior angle of a regular octagon?
A) 30 degrees
B) 45 degrees
C) 60 degrees
D) 72 degrees
Answer: B) 45 degrees

Q5. In a right-angled triangle, if one acute angle is 35 degrees, the other acute angle is:
A) 45 degrees
B) 55 degrees
C) 65 degrees
D) 145 degrees
Answer: B) 55 degrees

Q6. The lengths of two sides of a triangle are 7 cm and 10 cm. Which of the following can be the length of the third side?
A) 2 cm
B) 3 cm
C) 15 cm
D) 18 cm
Answer: C) 15 cm

Q7. In triangle ABC, angle B = 90 degrees. If AB = 6 cm and BC = 8 cm, the length of the hypotenuse AC is:
A) 10 cm
B) 12 cm
C) 14 cm
D) 16 cm
Answer: A) 10 cm

Q8. If the lengths of the sides of a triangle are 3 cm, 4 cm, and 5 cm, the radius of its circumcircle is:
A) 1.5 cm
B) 2 cm
C) 2.5 cm
D) 3 cm
Answer: C) 2.5 cm

Q9. A chord of length 16 cm is drawn in a circle of radius 10 cm. Find the distance of the chord from the center of the circle.
A) 4 cm
B) 5 cm
C) 6 cm
D) 8 cm
Answer: C) 6 cm

Q10. Angles subtended by the same arc at any point on the remaining part of the circle are:
A) Complementary
B) Supplementary
C) Equal
D) Unequal
Answer: C) Equal

Q11. In a circle, a central angle of 60 degrees subtends an arc of length 11 cm. What is the circumference of the circle?
A) 33 cm
B) 44 cm
C) 66 cm
D) 132 cm
Answer: C) 66 cm

Q12. Two circles with radii 5 cm and 3 cm touch each other externally. The distance between their centers is:
A) 2 cm
B) 4 cm
C) 8 cm
D) 15 cm
Answer: C) 8 cm

Q13. How many common tangents can be drawn to two intersecting circles?
A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 4
Answer: B) 2

Q14. If the area of a circle is 154 sq cm, its radius is (Take pi = 22/7):
A) 7 cm
B) 14 cm
C) 21 cm
D) 28 cm
Answer: A) 7 cm

Q15. The line joining the midpoints of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and is:
A) Equal to it
B) Half of it
C) One-third of it
D) Double of it
Answer: B) Half of it

Q16. The circumcenter of an obtuse-angled triangle lies:
A) Inside the triangle
B) Outside the triangle
C) On the longest side
D) On a vertex
Answer: B) Outside the triangle

Q17. In a cyclic quadrilateral ABCD, if angle A = 70 degrees, then the opposite angle C is:
A) 20 degrees
B) 70 degrees
C) 90 degrees
D) 110 degrees
Answer: D) 110 degrees

Q18. The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is equal to the ratio of the:
A) Corresponding sides
B) Corresponding altitudes
C) Squares of their corresponding sides
D) Cubes of their corresponding sides
Answer: C) Squares of their corresponding sides

Q19. An angle inscribed in a semicircle is always:
A) An acute angle
B) An obtuse angle
C) A right angle
D) A straight angle
Answer: C) A right angle
