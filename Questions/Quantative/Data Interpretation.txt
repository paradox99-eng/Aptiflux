Data Interpretation

Directions (Q1-Q5): Study the following table and answer the questions.
The table shows the sales of cars (in thousands) of three different models (A, B, and C) over 5 years.
Year    Model A    Model B    Model C
2018    40         50         60
2019    50         55         75
2020    60         60         80
2021    70         80         100
2022    80         90         110

Q1. What is the total number of Model B cars sold over all five years (in thousands)?
A) 315
B) 325
C) 335
D) 345
Answer: C) 335

Q2. What is the ratio of Model A sales in 2018 to Model C sales in 2021?
A) 2:5
B) 3:4
C) 4:7
D) 1:2
Answer: A) 2:5

Q3. What is the percentage increase in total car sales (all models combined) from 2020 to 2021?
A) 15%
B) 20%
C) 25%
D) 30%
Answer: C) 25%

Q4. In which year was the percentage increase in total car sales the highest compared to the previous year?
A) 2019
B) 2020
C) 2021
D) 2022
Answer: C) 2021

Q5. What is the average number of Model C cars sold per year (in thousands)?
A) 80
B) 85
C) 90
D) 95
Answer: B) 85


Directions (Q6-Q10): A student's monthly expenditure of $1000 is distributed as follows in a pie chart:
Rent: 30%, Food: 25%, Education: 20%, Entertainment: 15%, Savings: 10%.

Q6. What is the amount spent on Rent?
A) $200
B) $250
C) $300
D) $350
Answer: C) $300

Q7. How much more money is spent on Food than on Entertainment?
A) $50
B) $100
C) $150
D) $200
Answer: B) $100

Q8. If a pie chart is drawn, what will be the central angle corresponding to the expenditure on Education?
A) 36 degrees
B) 54 degrees
C) 72 degrees
D) 90 degrees
Answer: C) 72 degrees

Q9. What is the ratio of expenditure on Education to Savings?
A) 2:1
B) 3:1
C) 3:2
D) 4:1
Answer: A) 2:1

Q10. If the student's income increases by 20% next month and the percentage distribution of expenses remains exactly the same, what will be the new monthly Savings?
A) $100
B) $110
C) $120
D) $150
Answer: C) $120
