Ratio & Proportion

Q1. If A:B = 3:4 and B:C = 8:9, find A:C.
A) 1:2
B) 2:3
C) 3:4
D) 4:5
Answer: B) 2:3

Q2. Divide $1500 between A and B in the ratio 2:3. B's share is:
A) $600
B) $800
C) $900
D) $1000
Answer: C) $900

Q3. If 0.75 : x :: 5 : 8, find x.
A) 1.12
B) 1.2
C) 1.25
D) 1.5
Answer: B) 1.2

Q4. The fourth proportional to 5, 8, 15 is:
A) 20
B) 22
C) 24
D) 28
Answer: C) 24

Q5. Two numbers are in the ratio 3:5. If 9 is subtracted from each, the new ratio becomes 12:23. The smaller number is:
A) 27
B) 33
C) 36
D) 45
Answer: B) 33

Q6. The mean proportional between 9 and 25 is:
A) 12
B) 15
C) 18
D) 20
Answer: B) 15

Q7. The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 5:3. If there are 16 fewer girls than boys, how many total students are there?
A) 48
B) 56
C) 64
D) 80
Answer: C) 64

Q8. $1050 is divided among A, B, and C such that A gets 2/5 of what B and C together get. What is A's share?
A) $200
B) $300
C) $350
D) $400
Answer: B) $300

Q9. A bag contains coins of $1, 50c, and 25c in the ratio 1:2:4. If the total amount is $225, the number of 50c coins is:
A) 75
B) 100
C) 150
D) 200
Answer: C) 150

Q10. If x:y = 3:4, find the ratio (4x + 5y) : (5x - 2y).
A) 12:7
B) 24:7
C) 32:7
D) 36:7
Answer: C) 32:7

Q11. If A:B = 2:3, B:C = 4:5, and C:D = 6:7, find A:B:C:D.
A) 16:24:30:35
B) 16:22:30:35
C) 12:24:30:35
D) 18:24:30:35
Answer: A) 16:24:30:35

Q12. Salaries of Ravi and Sumit are in the ratio 2:3. If the salary of each is increased by $4000, the new ratio becomes 40:57. What is Sumit's present salary?
A) $17,000
B) $20,000
C) $25,500
D) $34,000
Answer: D) $34,000

Q13. The number 782 is divided into three parts in the ratio 1/2 : 2/3 : 3/4. The first part is:
A) 182
B) 190
C) 204
D) 212
Answer: C) 204

Q14. If 15% of x is the same as 20% of y, then x:y is:
A) 3:4
B) 4:3
C) 17:16
D) 16:17
Answer: B) 4:3

Q15. 15 machines can produce 500 units in 24 days. How many machines are required to produce 1000 units in 30 days?
A) 20
B) 24
C) 28
D) 30
Answer: B) 24

Q16. A mixture contains alcohol and water in the ratio 4:3. If 5 liters of water is added to the mixture, the ratio becomes 4:5. Find the quantity of alcohol in the given mixture.
A) 8 liters
B) 10 liters
C) 12 liters
D) 15 liters
Answer: B) 10 liters

Q17. If a:b:c = 3:4:7, then the ratio (a+b+c):c is equal to:
A) 2:1
B) 14:3
C) 7:2
D) 1:2
Answer: A) 2:1

Q18. The third proportional to 9 and 12 is:
A) 14
B) 15
C) 16
D) 18
Answer: C) 16

Q19. 45 people can complete a work in 16 days. If the work needs to be completed in 12 days, how many extra people are required?
A) 10
B) 12
C) 15
D) 20
Answer: C) 15

Q20. In a zoo, the ratio of lions to tigers is 4:5. If 8 new lions are brought in, the ratio becomes 6:5. How many tigers are there?
A) 15
B) 16
C) 20
D) 24
Answer: C) 20
