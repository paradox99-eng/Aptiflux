Sequence & Series

Q1. Find the next number in the sequence: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ...
A) 34
B) 35
C) 37
D) 39
Answer: C) 37

Q2. Find the 10th term of the Arithmetic Progression (A.P.): 3, 7, 11, 15, ...
A) 35
B) 39
C) 43
D) 47
Answer: B) 39

Q3. What is the sum of the first 20 terms of the Arithmetic Progression: 2, 4, 6, 8, ...?
A) 380
B) 400
C) 420
D) 440
Answer: C) 420

Q4. Find the next number in the sequence: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ...
A) 30
B) 32
C) 36
D) 49
Answer: C) 36

Q5. What is the 7th term of the Geometric Progression (G.P.): 2, 6, 18, 54, ...?
A) 486
B) 1458
C) 2187
D) 4374
Answer: B) 1458

Q6. Find the sum of the infinite Geometric Progression: 1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, ...
A) 1.5
B) 1.75
C) 2
D) Infinity
Answer: C) 2

Q7. In an Arithmetic Progression, if the 5th term is 14 and the 12th term is 35, find the common difference.
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 5
Answer: B) 3

Q8. Find the missing term in the series: 3, 8, 15, 24, __, 48.
A) 32
B) 34
C) 35
D) 36
Answer: C) 35

Q9. If the sum of the first n natural numbers is 55, what is the value of n?
A) 9
B) 10
C) 11
D) 12
Answer: B) 10

Q10. The sum of three numbers in an A.P. is 21 and their product is 280. Find the numbers.
A) 3, 7, 11
B) 4, 7, 10
C) 5, 7, 9
D) 2, 7, 12
Answer: B) 4, 7, 10

Q11. What is the sum of the first 10 terms of the Geometric Progression: 1, 2, 4, 8, ...?
A) 511
B) 512
C) 1023
D) 1024
Answer: C) 1023

Q12. The 3rd term of a G.P. is 12 and the 6th term is 96. What is the common ratio?
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 8
Answer: A) 2

Q13. How many terms are there in the Arithmetic Progression: 5, 9, 13, ..., 81?
A) 18
B) 19
C) 20
D) 21
Answer: C) 20

Q14. Find the next number in the Fibonacci sequence: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ...
A) 18
B) 20
C) 21
D) 24
Answer: C) 21

Q15. The sum of an infinite geometric progression is 4 and the first term is 3. What is the common ratio?
A) 1/2
B) 1/3
C) 1/4
D) 3/4
Answer: C) 1/4

Q16. Find the 20th term of the sequence defined by the formula a_n = n^2 - 2n.
A) 360
B) 380
C) 400
D) 420
Answer: A) 360

Q17. Insert two arithmetic means between 10 and 22.
A) 13, 16
B) 14, 18
C) 12, 17
D) 15, 19
Answer: B) 14, 18

Q18. The geometric mean of 4 and 16 is:
A) 8
B) 10
C) 12
D) 32
Answer: A) 8

Q19. Find the sum of the series 1^2 + 2^2 + 3^2 + ... + 10^2.
A) 350
B) 385
C) 425
D) 500
Answer: B) 385

Q20. In a sequence, the n-th term is given by a_n = (-1)^n * n. What is the sum of the first 100 terms?
A) 0
B) 50
C) -50
D) 100
Answer: B) 50
