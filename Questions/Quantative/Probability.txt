Probability

Q1. What is the probability of getting a head when a fair coin is tossed?
A) 1/4
B) 1/3
C) 1/2
D) 1
Answer: C) 1/2

Q2. Two fair coins are tossed simultaneously. What is the probability of getting at least one head?
A) 1/4
B) 1/2
C) 3/4
D) 1
Answer: C) 3/4

Q3. A fair die is rolled. What is the probability of getting a prime number?
A) 1/6
B) 1/3
C) 1/2
D) 2/3
Answer: C) 1/2

Q4. What is the probability of drawing an ace from a well-shuffled standard deck of 52 playing cards?
A) 1/13
B) 1/26
C) 1/52
D) 4/13
Answer: A) 1/13

Q5. Two dice are rolled simultaneously. What is the probability that the sum of the numbers on the dice is exactly 8?
A) 1/6
B) 5/36
C) 1/9
D) 1/12
Answer: B) 5/36

Q6. A bag contains 4 red balls, 3 green balls, and 5 blue balls. If one ball is drawn at random, what is the probability that it is green?
A) 1/4
B) 1/3
C) 1/2
D) 3/4
Answer: A) 1/4

Q7. In a lottery, there are 10 prizes and 25 blanks. What is the probability of getting a prize?
A) 1/10
B) 2/5
C) 2/7
D) 5/7
Answer: C) 2/7

Q8. What is the probability that a non-leap year selected at random will contain exactly 53 Sundays?
A) 1/7
B) 2/7
C) 1/365
D) 53/365
Answer: A) 1/7

Q9. A ticket is drawn at random from 50 tickets numbered 1 to 50. What is the probability that the drawn ticket is a multiple of 5?
A) 1/10
B) 1/5
C) 1/4
D) 1/2
Answer: B) 1/5

Q10. From a pack of 52 cards, two cards are drawn at random. What is the probability that both are kings?
A) 1/169
B) 1/221
C) 1/26
D) 3/221
Answer: B) 1/221

Q11. A box contains 20 electric bulbs, out of which 4 are defective. Two bulbs are chosen at random from this box. What is the probability that at least one of them is defective?
A) 7/19
B) 12/19
C) 1/19
D) 4/19
Answer: A) 7/19

Q12. What is the probability of getting a sum of 9 from two throws of a standard die?
A) 1/6
B) 1/8
C) 1/9
D) 1/12
Answer: C) 1/9

Q13. Three coins are tossed together. What is the probability of getting exactly two tails?
A) 1/8
B) 1/4
C) 3/8
D) 1/2
Answer: C) 3/8

Q14. The probability that it will rain tomorrow is 0.85. What is the probability that it will not rain tomorrow?
A) 0.05
B) 0.15
C) 0.25
D) 0.85
Answer: B) 0.15

Q15. Two friends X and Y appeared for an exam. The probability of selection of X is 1/7 and that of Y is 2/9. What is the probability that both of them are selected?
A) 1/63
B) 2/63
C) 3/63
D) 1/7
Answer: B) 2/63

Q16. A bag contains 6 black and 8 white balls. One ball is drawn at random. What is the probability that the ball drawn is white?
A) 3/7
B) 4/7
C) 1/8
D) 3/4
Answer: B) 4/7

Q17. What is the probability of drawing a face card (Jack, Queen, or King) from a standard deck of 52 cards?
A) 1/13
B) 3/13
C) 4/13
D) 1/4
Answer: B) 3/13

Q18. If the probability of an event happening is p, then the probability of the event not happening is:
A) 0
B) 1
C) p - 1
D) 1 - p
Answer: D) 1 - p

Q19. Two dice are thrown together. What is the probability that the sum of the numbers on the two faces is divisible by 4 or 6?
A) 1/4
B) 1/3
C) 7/18
D) 5/18
Answer: C) 7/18

Q20. A jar contains 3 red, 4 blue, and 5 green marbles. If two marbles are drawn one by one without replacement, what is the probability that both are blue?
A) 1/12
B) 1/11
C) 2/11
D) 1/6
Answer: B) 1/11
