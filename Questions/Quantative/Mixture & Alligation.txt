Mixture & Alligation

Q1. In what ratio must a grocer mix two varieties of pulses costing $15 and $20 per kg respectively so as to get a mixture worth $16.50 per kg?
A) 3:7
B) 5:7
C) 7:3
D) 7:5
Answer: C) 7:3

Q2. Find the ratio in which rice at $7.20 a kg be mixed with rice at $5.70 a kg to produce a mixture worth $6.30 a kg.
A) 1:3
B) 2:3
C) 3:4
D) 4:5
Answer: B) 2:3

Q3. A mixture of 150 liters of wine and water contains 20% water. How much more water should be added so that water becomes 25% of the new mixture?
A) 10 liters
B) 15 liters
C) 20 liters
D) 25 liters
Answer: A) 10 liters

Q4. In what ratio must water be mixed with milk to gain 16 2/3% on selling the mixture at cost price?
A) 1:4
B) 1:5
C) 1:6
D) 2:3
Answer: C) 1:6

Q5. A vessel is filled with liquid, 3 parts of which are water and 5 parts syrup. How much of the mixture must be drawn off and replaced with water so that the mixture may be half water and half syrup?
A) 1/3
B) 1/4
C) 1/5
D) 1/7
Answer: C) 1/5

Q6. A can contains a mixture of two liquids A and B in the ratio 7:5. When 9 liters of mixture are drawn off and the can is filled with B, the ratio of A and B becomes 7:9. How many liters of liquid A was contained by the can initially?
A) 10 liters
B) 20 liters
C) 21 liters
D) 25 liters
Answer: C) 21 liters

Q7. A container contains 40 liters of milk. From this container 4 liters of milk was taken out and replaced by water. This process was repeated further two times. How much milk is now contained by the container?
A) 26.34 liters
B) 27.36 liters
C) 28 liters
D) 29.16 liters
Answer: D) 29.16 liters

Q8. 8 liters are drawn from a cask full of wine and is then filled with water. This operation is performed three more times. The ratio of the quantity of wine now left in the cask to that of water is 16:65. How much wine did the cask hold originally?
A) 18 liters
B) 24 liters
C) 32 liters
D) 42 liters
Answer: B) 24 liters

Q9. A merchant has 1000 kg of sugar, part of which he sells at 8% profit and the rest at 18% profit. He gains 14% on the whole. The quantity sold at 18% profit is:
A) 400 kg
B) 560 kg
C) 600 kg
D) 640 kg
Answer: C) 600 kg

Q10. In what ratio must tea at $62 per kg be mixed with tea at $72 per kg so that the mixture must be worth $64.50 per kg?
A) 3:1
B) 3:2
C) 4:3
D) 5:3
Answer: A) 3:1

Q11. How many kilograms of sugar costing $9 per kg must be mixed with 27 kg of sugar costing $7 per kg so that there may be a gain of 10% by selling the mixture at $9.24 per kg?
A) 36 kg
B) 42 kg
C) 54 kg
D) 63 kg
Answer: D) 63 kg

Q12. Milk and water in two vessels A and B are in the ratio 4:3 and 2:3 respectively. In what ratio should the liquids in both the vessels be mixed to obtain a new mixture in vessel C containing half milk and half water?
A) 7:5
B) 5:7
C) 1:1
D) 2:3
Answer: A) 7:5

Q13. The cost of Type 1 rice is $15 per kg and Type 2 rice is $20 per kg. If both Type 1 and Type 2 are mixed in the ratio of 2:3, then the price per kg of the mixed variety of rice is:
A) $17.00
B) $17.50
C) $18.00
D) $18.50
Answer: C) $18.00

Q14. Two vessels A and B contain spirit and water mixed in the ratio 5:2 and 7:6 respectively. Find the ratio in which these mixtures be mixed to obtain a new mixture in vessel C containing spirit and water in the ratio 8:5.
A) 4:3
B) 5:6
C) 7:9
D) 9:7
Answer: C) 7:9

Q15. 20 liters of a mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 5:3. If 4 liters of this mixture be replaced by 4 liters of pure milk, the ratio of milk to water in the new mixture would be:
A) 2:1
B) 6:3
C) 7:3
D) 8:3
Answer: C) 7:3

Q16. A jar full of whisky contains 40% alcohol. A part of this whisky is replaced by another containing 19% alcohol and now the percentage of alcohol was found to be 26%. The fraction of the quantity of whisky replaced is:
A) 1/3
B) 2/3
C) 2/5
D) 3/5
Answer: B) 2/3

Q17. A mixture of 40 liters of milk and water contains 10% water. How much water must be added to make 20% water in the new mixture?
A) 4 liters
B) 5 liters
C) 6.5 liters
D) 7.5 liters
Answer: B) 5 liters

Q18. An alloy contains zinc, copper and tin in the ratio 2:3:1 and another contains copper, tin and lead in the ratio 5:4:3. If equal weights of both alloys are melted together to form a third alloy, then the weight of lead per kg in the new alloy will be:
A) 1/2 kg
B) 1/4 kg
C) 1/8 kg
D) 3/14 kg
Answer: C) 1/8 kg

Q19. 300 grams of a sugar solution has 40% sugar in it. How much sugar should be added to make it 50% in the solution?
A) 10 grams
B) 40 grams
C) 60 grams
D) 80 grams
Answer: C) 60 grams

Q20. Two casks of 40 liters and 35 liters are filled with mixtures of wine and water, the proportions in the two casks being respectively 3:1 and 4:3. If the contents of the two casks are mixed and 25 liters of pure water is added to the whole, what will be the proportion of wine to water in the resultant mixture?
A) 1:1
B) 2:1
C) 3:2
D) 5:4
Answer: A) 1:1
