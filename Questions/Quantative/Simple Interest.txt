Simple Interest

Q1. What is the simple interest on $5000 at 8% per annum for 3 years?
A) $1000
B) $1200
C) $1400
D) $1600
Answer: B) $1200

Q2. A sum of money doubles itself in 10 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?
A) 8%
B) 10%
C) 12%
D) 15%
Answer: B) 10%

Q3. At what rate percent per annum will a sum of money triple itself in 16 years?
A) 10%
B) 12.5%
C) 15%
D) 20%
Answer: B) 12.5%

Q4. Find the principal if the simple interest earned at 5% p.a. for 4 years is $800.
A) $3000
B) $3500
C) $4000
D) $4500
Answer: C) $4000

Q5. A sum was put at simple interest at a certain rate for 3 years. Had it been put at a 2% higher rate, it would have fetched $360 more. Find the sum.
A) $4000
B) $5000
C) $6000
D) $7500
Answer: C) $6000

Q6. In what time will $1200 amount to $1560 at 10% p.a. simple interest?
A) 2 years
B) 3 years
C) 4 years
D) 5 years
Answer: B) 3 years

Q7. A certain sum amounts to $1008 in 2 years and to $1164 in 3.5 years. Find the principal.
A) $700
B) $750
C) $800
D) $850
Answer: C) $800

Q8. What will be the ratio of simple interest earned by a certain amount at the same rate of interest for 6 years and 9 years?
A) 1:3
B) 1:4
C) 2:3
D) 3:4
Answer: C) 2:3

Q9. A sum amounts to $2800 in 3 years and $3200 in 5 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest?
A) 7.5%
B) 8.5%
C) 9.09%
D) 10%
Answer: C) 9.09%

Q10. Reena took a loan of $1200 with simple interest for as many years as the rate of interest. If she paid $432 as interest, what was the rate?
A) 4%
B) 5%
C) 6%
D) 8%
Answer: C) 6%

Q11. If the simple interest on a certain sum of money for 3 years at 5% is $150, find the sum.
A) $800
B) $900
C) $1000
D) $1200
Answer: C) $1000

Q12. A lent $5000 to B for 2 years and $3000 to C for 4 years on simple interest at the same rate of interest, and received $2200 in all from both of them as interest. The rate of interest per annum is:
A) 5%
B) 8%
C) 10%
D) 12%
Answer: C) 10%

Q13. The simple interest on a sum of money is 1/9 of the principal, and the number of years is equal to the rate percent per annum. The rate percent is:
A) 3%
B) 3.33%
C) 4.5%
D) 9%
Answer: B) 3.33%

Q14. A man took a loan from a bank at the rate of 12% p.a. simple interest. After 3 years he had to pay $5400 interest only for the period. The principal amount borrowed by him was:
A) $10000
B) $12000
C) $15000
D) $20000
Answer: C) $15000

Q15. An amount is lent at 15% p.a. simple interest for 2 years. If the same amount was lent at 20% p.a. simple interest for the same time, the interest would be $500 more. What is the principal amount?
A) $4000
B) $4500
C) $5000
D) $6000
Answer: C) $5000

Q16. The simple interest on a certain sum for 8 months at 4% per annum is $129 less than the simple interest on the same sum for 15 months at 5% per annum. The sum is:
A) $2580
B) $3000
C) $3200
D) $3600
Answer: D) $3600

Q17. What sum of money will amount to $520 in 5 years and to $568 in 7 years at simple interest?
A) $350
B) $380
C) $400
D) $450
Answer: C) $400

Q18. If the annual rate of simple interest increases from 10% to 12.5%, a man's yearly income increases by $1250. His principal is:
A) $40000
B) $45000
C) $50000
D) $60000
Answer: C) $50000

Q19. A sum of $1600 gives a simple interest of $252 in 2 years and 3 months. The rate of interest per annum is:
A) 5.5%
B) 6%
C) 7%
D) 8%
Answer: C) 7%

Q20. A person borrows $5000 for 2 years at 4% p.a. simple interest. He immediately lends it to another person at 6.25% p.a. for 2 years. Find his gain in the transaction per year.
A) $112.50
B) $125.00
C) $225.00
D) $250.00
Answer: A) $112.50
