Percentage

Q1. If 20% of a number is 60, what is 50% of that same number?
A) 100
B) 120
C) 150
D) 300
Answer: C) 150

Q2. A worker's salary was increased by 20% and then subsequently decreased by 20%. What is the net change in his salary?
A) 0% (No change)
B) 2% decrease
C) 4% decrease
D) 4% increase
Answer: C) 4% decrease

Q3. In an election between two candidates, the winner got 60% of the valid votes and won by a majority of 2000 votes. Find the total number of valid votes.
A) 8000
B) 10000
C) 12000
D) 15000
Answer: B) 10000

Q4. What percentage is equivalent to the fraction 3/8?
A) 33.33%
B) 37.5%
C) 62.5%
D) 75%
Answer: B) 37.5%

Q5. The population of a town increases by 10% annually. If the present population is 10,000, what will it be after 2 years?
A) 12000
B) 12100
C) 12200
D) 12500
Answer: B) 12100

Q6. A student needs 40% marks to pass an exam. He gets 178 marks and fails by 22 marks. What are the maximum marks for the exam?
A) 400
B) 500
C) 600
D) 800
Answer: B) 500

Q7. If the price of sugar increases by 25%, by what percentage must a household decrease its consumption so that their monthly expenditure remains the same?
A) 15%
B) 20%
C) 25%
D) 30%
Answer: B) 20%

Q8. Two numbers are respectively 20% and 50% more than a third number. The ratio of the two numbers is:
A) 2:5
B) 4:5
C) 5:4
D) 6:7
Answer: B) 4:5

Q9. Ravi spends 30% of his income on food, 20% on rent, and saves the rest. If his savings are $2500, what is his total income?
A) $4000
B) $5000
C) $6000
D) $7500
Answer: B) $5000

Q10. In a class, 60% of students like Math, 50% like Science, and 20% like both. What percentage of students like neither?
A) 10%
B) 20%
C) 30%
D) 40%
Answer: A) 10%

Q11. If A's height is 10% more than B's height, by how much percent is B's height less than that of A?
A) 9.09%
B) 10%
C) 10.01%
D) 11.11%
Answer: A) 9.09%

Q12. 65% of a number is 21 less than 4/5 of that number. What is the number?
A) 100
B) 120
C) 140
D) 160
Answer: C) 140

Q13. The price of an article is cut by 20%. To restore it to its original price, the new price must be increased by:
A) 15%
B) 20%
C) 22.5%
D) 25%
Answer: D) 25%

Q14. Half of 1 percent, written as a decimal, is:
A) 0.005
B) 0.05
C) 0.5
D) 5.0
Answer: A) 0.005

Q15. What is 30% of 28% of 480?
A) 38.42
B) 40.32
C) 42.16
D) 45.50
Answer: B) 40.32

Q16. If 15% of x is the same as 20% of y, then x:y is:
A) 3:4
B) 4:3
C) 15:20
D) 20:15
Answer: B) 4:3

Q17. In a test, a candidate scored 30% marks and failed by 15 marks. Another candidate scored 40% marks and got 35 marks more than the minimum passing marks. The maximum marks for the test are:
A) 400
B) 450
C) 500
D) 600
Answer: C) 500

Q18. The length of a rectangle is increased by 30% and its breadth is decreased by 20%. What is the percentage change in its area?
A) 4% decrease
B) 4% increase
C) 10% decrease
D) 10% increase
Answer: B) 4% increase

Q19. If 5% of A = B, then B% of 20 is the same as:
A) 1% of A
B) 2% of A
C) 5% of A
D) 20% of A
Answer: A) 1% of A

Q20. In an examination, 35% of the total students failed in Hindi, 45% failed in English, and 20% failed in both. The percentage of those who passed in both subjects is:
A) 10%
B) 20%
C) 30%
D) 40%
Answer: D) 40%
