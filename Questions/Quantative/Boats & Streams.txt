Boats & Streams

Q1. A boat can travel with a speed of 13 km/hr in still water. If the speed of the stream is 4 km/hr, find the time taken by the boat to go 68 km downstream.
A) 2 hours
B) 3 hours
C) 4 hours
D) 5 hours
Answer: C) 4 hours

Q2. A man's speed with the current is 15 km/hr and the speed of the current is 2.5 km/hr. The man's speed against the current is:
A) 8.5 km/hr
B) 9 km/hr
C) 10 km/hr
D) 12.5 km/hr
Answer: C) 10 km/hr

Q3. A motorboat, whose speed is 15 km/hr in still water goes 30 km downstream and comes back in a total of 4 hours 30 minutes. The speed of the stream is:
A) 4 km/hr
B) 5 km/hr
C) 6 km/hr
D) 10 km/hr
Answer: B) 5 km/hr

Q4. A man can row upstream at 8 km/hr and downstream at 13 km/hr. The speed of the stream is:
A) 2.5 km/hr
B) 4.2 km/hr
C) 5 km/hr
D) 10.5 km/hr
Answer: A) 2.5 km/hr

Q5. If a boat goes 7 km upstream in 42 minutes and the speed of the stream is 3 km/hr, then the speed of the boat in still water is:
A) 4.2 km/hr
B) 9 km/hr
C) 13 km/hr
D) 21 km/hr
Answer: C) 13 km/hr

Q6. A boat covers a certain distance downstream in 1 hour, while it comes back in 1.5 hours. If the speed of the stream is 3 km/hr, what is the speed of the boat in still water?
A) 12 km/hr
B) 13 km/hr
C) 14 km/hr
D) 15 km/hr
Answer: D) 15 km/hr

Q7. A boatman goes 2 km against the current of the stream in 1 hour and goes 1 km along the current in 10 minutes. How long will it take to go 5 km in stationary water?
A) 1 hr
B) 1 hr 15 mins
C) 1 hr 30 mins
D) 1 hr 45 mins
Answer: B) 1 hr 15 mins

Q8. In one hour, a boat goes 11 km/hr along the stream and 5 km/hr against the stream. The speed of the boat in still water is:
A) 3 km/hr
B) 5 km/hr
C) 8 km/hr
D) 9 km/hr
Answer: C) 8 km/hr

Q9. A man rows to a place 48 km distant and comes back in 14 hours. He finds that he can row 4 km with the stream in the same time as 3 km against the stream. The rate of the stream is:
A) 1 km/hr
B) 1.5 km/hr
C) 2 km/hr
D) 2.5 km/hr
Answer: A) 1 km/hr

Q10. A boat takes 90 minutes less to travel 36 miles downstream than to travel the same distance upstream. If the speed of the boat in still water is 10 mph, the speed of the stream is:
A) 2 mph
B) 2.5 mph
C) 3 mph
D) 4 mph
Answer: A) 2 mph

Q11. A man can row at 5 km/hr in still water. If the velocity of the current is 1 km/hr and it takes him 1 hour to row to a place and come back, how far is the place?
A) 2.4 km
B) 2.5 km
C) 3 km
D) 3.6 km
Answer: A) 2.4 km

Q12. The speed of a boat in still water is 15 km/hr and the rate of current is 3 km/hr. The distance travelled downstream in 12 minutes is:
A) 1.2 km
B) 1.8 km
C) 2.4 km
D) 3.6 km
Answer: D) 3.6 km

Q13. A boat running upstream takes 8 hours 48 minutes to cover a certain distance, while it takes 4 hours to cover the same distance running downstream. What is the ratio between the speed of the boat and speed of the water current respectively?
A) 2:1
B) 3:2
C) 8:3
D) Cannot be determined
Answer: C) 8:3

Q14. A boat goes 24 km upstream and 28 km downstream in 6 hours. It goes 30 km upstream and 21 km downstream in 6 hours 30 minutes. The speed of the boat in still water is:
A) 8 km/hr
B) 9 km/hr
C) 10 km/hr
D) 12 km/hr
Answer: C) 10 km/hr

Q15. A man can row 18 km/hr in still water. It takes him thrice as long to row up as to row down the river. Find the rate of stream.
A) 6 km/hr
B) 9 km/hr
C) 12 km/hr
D) 15 km/hr
Answer: B) 9 km/hr

Q16. A man can row 30 km downstream and return in a total of 8 hours. If the speed of the boat in still water is four times the speed of the current, then the speed of the current is:
A) 1 km/hr
B) 2 km/hr
C) 3 km/hr
D) 4 km/hr
Answer: B) 2 km/hr

Q17. A boat rows 12 km upstream in 4 hours. If the speed of the current is 1.5 km/hr, how much time will it take to row the same distance downstream?
A) 2 hours
B) 2.5 hours
C) 3 hours
D) 3.5 hours
Answer: A) 2 hours

Q18. The speed of a boat in still water is 10 km/hr. If it can travel 26 km downstream and 14 km upstream in the same time, the speed of the stream is:
A) 2 km/hr
B) 2.5 km/hr
C) 3 km/hr
D) 4 km/hr
Answer: C) 3 km/hr

Q19. A man can row 9 1/3 km/hr in still water and finds that it takes him thrice as much time to row up than as to row down the same distance in the river. The speed of the current is:
A) 3 1/3 km/hr
B) 4 km/hr
C) 4 2/3 km/hr
D) 5 km/hr
Answer: C) 4 2/3 km/hr

Q20. A boat goes 40 km upstream in 8 hours and a distance of 36 km downstream in 6 hours. The speed of the boat in still water is:
A) 4.5 km/hr
B) 5 km/hr
C) 5.5 km/hr
D) 6 km/hr
Answer: C) 5.5 km/hr
