Permutation & Combination

Q1. Evaluate the value of 5! (5 factorial).
A) 24
B) 60
C) 120
D) 720
Answer: C) 120

Q2. In how many ways can the letters of the word "APPLE" be arranged?
A) 60
B) 120
C) 360
D) 720
Answer: A) 60

Q3. In how many ways can 5 persons be seated in a row?
A) 25
B) 60
C) 120
D) 240
Answer: C) 120

Q4. In how many ways can 6 people be seated around a circular table?
A) 120
B) 360
C) 720
D) 5040
Answer: A) 120

Q5. From a group of 7 men and 6 women, 5 persons are to be selected to form a committee. In how many ways can it be done so that the committee consists of exactly 3 men and 2 women?
A) 315
B) 420
C) 525
D) 630
Answer: C) 525

Q6. How many 3-digit numbers can be formed using the digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 if repetition of digits is not allowed?
A) 20
B) 60
C) 120
D) 125
Answer: B) 60

Q7. In how many ways can the letters of the word "LEADER" be arranged?
A) 120
B) 360
C) 720
D) 1440
Answer: B) 360

Q8. In how many ways can a selection of 3 consonants and 2 vowels be made from 7 consonants and 4 vowels?
A) 105
B) 210
C) 420
D) 840
Answer: B) 210

Q9. A bag contains 2 white balls, 3 black balls and 4 red balls. In how many ways can 3 balls be drawn from the bag?
A) 64
B) 72
C) 84
D) 120
Answer: C) 84

Q10. In how many ways can the letters of the word "MACHINE" be arranged so that the vowels may occupy only the odd positions?
A) 144
B) 288
C) 576
D) 720
Answer: C) 576

Q11. How many two-digit numbers can be formed from the digits 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 without repetition?
A) 16
B) 20
C) 24
D) 25
Answer: A) 16

Q12. Evaluate the value of 8C3 (8 choose 3).
A) 24
B) 56
C) 112
D) 336
Answer: B) 56

Q13. There are 10 points in a plane, out of which 4 are collinear. How many straight lines can be formed by joining these points?
A) 40
B) 41
C) 45
D) 46
Answer: A) 40

Q14. There are 10 points in a plane, out of which 4 are collinear. How many triangles can be formed using these points?
A) 110
B) 112
C) 116
D) 120
Answer: C) 116

Q15. In a party, every person shakes hands with every other person. If there are a total of 105 handshakes, how many persons were there in the party?
A) 13
B) 14
C) 15
D) 16
Answer: C) 15

Q16. How many words can be formed from the letters of the word "SIGNATURE" so that the vowels always come together?
A) 4320
B) 17280
C) 34560
D) 40320
Answer: B) 17280

Q17. In how many ways can 4 boys and 3 girls be seated in a row so that no two girls sit together?
A) 144
B) 288
C) 1440
D) 5040
Answer: C) 1440

Q18. If nP2 = 30, what is the value of n?
A) 5
B) 6
C) 7
D) 15
Answer: B) 6

Q19. How many diagonals can be drawn in a decagon (a polygon with 10 sides)?
A) 35
B) 40
C) 45
D) 90
Answer: A) 35

Q20. From a class of 15 students, a captain and a vice-captain are to be chosen. In how many ways can this be done?
A) 30
B) 105
C) 210
D) 225
Answer: C) 210
