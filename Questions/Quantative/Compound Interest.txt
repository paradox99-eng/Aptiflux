Compound Interest

Q1. Find the compound interest on $10,000 for 2 years at 10% p.a.
A) $2000
B) $2100
C) $2200
D) $2300
Answer: B) $2100

Q2. The difference between Compound Interest and Simple Interest on a sum for 2 years at 5% p.a. is $25. The sum is:
A) $8000
B) $9000
C) $10000
D) $12000
Answer: C) $10000

Q3. A sum of money doubles itself in 4 years at compound interest. In how many years will it amount to 8 times itself?
A) 8 years
B) 10 years
C) 12 years
D) 16 years
Answer: C) 12 years

Q4. What is the compound interest on $5000 for 1 year at 8% p.a., compounded half-yearly?
A) $400
B) $404
C) $408
D) $416
Answer: C) $408

Q5. A sum amounts to $1331 in 3 years at 10% p.a. compounded annually. Find the sum.
A) $1000
B) $1100
C) $1200
D) $1250
Answer: A) $1000

Q6. At what rate percent per annum will $2000 amount to $2420 in 2 years at compound interest?
A) 8%
B) 10%
C) 12%
D) 15%
Answer: B) 10%

Q7. The compound interest on a certain sum for 2 years is $410 and the simple interest is $400. Find the rate of interest.
A) 4%
B) 5%
C) 6%
D) 8%
Answer: B) 5%

Q8. Find the amount of $8000 in 1.5 years at 10% p.a., interest being compounded half-yearly.
A) $9120
B) $9261
C) $9300
D) $9450
Answer: B) $9261

Q9. If a sum amounts to $4500 in 2 years and $6750 in 4 years at compound interest, find the sum.
A) $2000
B) $2500
C) $3000
D) $3500
Answer: C) $3000

Q10. A tree increases annually by 1/8 of its height. By how much will it increase after 2 years if it stands today 64 cm high?
A) 15 cm
B) 16 cm
C) 17 cm
D) 18 cm
Answer: C) 17 cm

Q11. The present worth of $169 due in 2 years at 4% per annum compound interest is:
A) $150.25
B) $154.75
C) $156.25
D) $158.00
Answer: C) $156.25

Q12. If the compound interest on a certain sum for 2 years at 3% per annum is $101.50, then the simple interest on the same sum at the same rate and for the same time will be:
A) $90.00
B) $95.50
C) $98.25
D) $100.00
Answer: D) $100.00

Q13. The difference between compound interest and simple interest on a certain sum of money for 3 years at 10% per annum is $31. Find the sum.
A) $500
B) $750
C) $1000
D) $1250
Answer: C) $1000

Q14. A builder borrows $2550 to be paid back with compound interest at the rate of 4% per annum by the end of 2 years in two equal yearly installments. How much will each installment be?
A) $1275
B) $1283
C) $1325
D) $1352
Answer: D) $1352

Q15. Find the compound interest on $16000 at 20% per annum for 9 months, compounded quarterly.
A) $2500
B) $2522
C) $2545
D) $2600
Answer: B) $2522

Q16. The population of a city increases at the rate of 5% p.a. If the present population is 185,220, what was its population 3 years ago?
A) 150,000
B) 160,000
C) 165,000
D) 170,000
Answer: B) 160,000

Q17. Albert invested an amount of $8000 in a fixed deposit scheme for 2 years at a compound interest rate of 5% p.a. How much amount will Albert get on maturity of the fixed deposit?
A) $8600
B) $8620
C) $8800
D) $8820
Answer: D) $8820

Q18. The difference between simple and compound interests compounded annually on a certain sum of money for 2 years at 4% per annum is $1. The sum is:
A) $600
B) $625
C) $650
D) $700
Answer: B) $625

Q19. What is the difference between the compound interests on $5000 for 1.5 years at 4% per annum compounded yearly and half-yearly?
A) $2.04
B) $3.06
C) $4.80
D) $8.30
Answer: A) $2.04

Q20. A sum of money becomes $13,380 after 3 years and $20,070 after 6 years on compound interest. The sum is:
A) $8800
B) $8900
C) $8920
D) $9040
Answer: C) $8920
